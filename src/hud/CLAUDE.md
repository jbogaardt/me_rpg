# src/hud: HTML overlays

The HUD, dialog, menu, shop, trade window, map overlay and loading screen are **DOM, not Pixi**, so text stays sharp on
phones and respects safe-area insets; markup in `index.html`, styles in `src/ui.css`. The game keeps a single `<canvas>`
(the smoke tests rely on it): the map is painted on a detached canvas and shown as an `<img>`.

- **Reuse the UI kit classes** (`.panel`, `.wood`, `.paper`, plank tabs, square buttons; Lamoot's RPG GUI kit, CC-BY, see
  CREDITS.md, cut by `tools/ui/cut-gui.ts`), `.pix` (notched pixel chip: tracker, level badge, DM badge) and the two fonts rather than inventing styles: **Pixelify Sans** (OFL, the one pixel face: HUD, titles, tabs, banners, chips, and the world's `FloatText` labels) and Crimson Text (long body text). Pixel art is code (`heartArt.ts`: ASCII grid to SVG); text over the world uses `--outline`.
- Build text with `textContent`, never `innerHTML`, for anything that came from a player or content.
- **Item pictures** are `iconSvg(kind, art)` (`itemIcons.ts`): the item's `art` cell of the item sheet (`systems/itemArt.ts`, built by `tools/sprites/itemSheet.ts`, a CSS background of `--item-sheet`), else the SVG fallback; presentation only, no co-op traffic; anything eaten or drunk is the `food` kind. Ground items and relics use the same cells in Pixi (`entities/itemArt.ts`).
- A new tappable overlay must be added to the touch allow-list (`src/core/CLAUDE.md`); a new button is a `data-key` element.
- **Dialog box vs. DM caption** (`Dialog`): the box says *conversations* (NPC, quest brief, sign: paged, the player stands
  still, the next waits in line, `pauses` holds the world for everyone). The DM's words are never a dialog: they
  are a *caption* (`#dm-caption`), plain text that takes **no input**, never stops play and leaves by itself; each page also speaks it, with the browser's voice, or the Kokoro model only when chosen in the Controls tab (`voice.ts`, `naturalVoice.ts`, `voiceChoice.ts`: ~90 MB, never the default; the browser voice covers until ready and on failure, said once; local, mutable with 🔊). Say new DM words through `tell`, not another overlay; it also cuts director cues (`<cue:.../>`, `docs/dungeon-master.md`) out of any text and, for a line that has some, types it at reading pace, handing each cue to `onCue`.
- **Popups only when the player starts a conversation** (an NPC, a sign, a shop, the DM in person: walk up and press the action key). Everything told unasked (a defeat, a DM quest, a chapter's note, a trial's result) is *passive text*: `Dialog.note`, the DM caption. Never `say` for those.
- **Item pickups are a toast, never a card** (`PickupToasts`, rules in `toastStack.ts`): a small see-through stack at the left edge, merged repeats, gone in about 2 s (1 s with a foe near), plus a chime (`playPing`). Local, nothing is sent.
- **Combat sounds** (`gameSfx.ts`, event table in `systems/gameSounds.ts`; core engine, cosmetic): swing, hit, hurt, death, bolt, chest, level-up, pickup, a foe's windup `tell` and `deny`, procedural, hooked in `Visuals`, so the host, solo and every guest play from the events they already get (no protocol change; one event is one sound). Separate from the DM's cue vocabulary (`sfx.ts`) and the voice mute: own mute and volume in the Controls tab (`SoundSetting`, `core/soundSettings.ts`). A new sound is a name, a recipe and a table line.
- **The map overlay is local and always available** (opaque parchment in the menu's frame, the legend under it, the HUD buttons stay uncovered): it fills in as the players in view explore (`map/explored.ts`),
  nothing is sent; a solo game saves it with the rest (`Atlas.pack`). The button and M key never go missing (host, guest, DM, touch, any zone); a room has no overview (its name and a note, `MapOverlay.setRoom`). Only the aids on it are optional: the `guide`
  capability (search circle, star, tracker line; its `fog` setting shows the whole zone) and the `compass` capability,
  each also switchable by the player in the Controls tab (`AidSetting`, `systems/aids.ts`).
- **Partner arrows** (`PartnerArrows.ts`, maths in `edgeArrow.ts`; core engine, online only): an edge-of-screen arrow in the
  partner's colour with the distance in tiles for a partner in this zone but out of view, and a chip for one in another zone.
  Nothing is sent: a guest has every in-zone player in `snap.players`, the rest in `away`.
- **The quiet HUD** (`Hud.ts`, every threshold and rule in `systems/hudRules.ts`; core engine, cosmetic, local, no co-op traffic): health is pixel hearts (10 HP each, doubling past ten hearts, quarter fills), no numbers: they fade to 35% after 3 s unchanged at 60% HP or more, return on any change, stay full under 40%, and the lost one pops (not for reduced motion). Mana and needs are thin bars shown only after a spend or bite, or when low; gold flashes 2 s. Exact numbers are in the Menu. Foe bars: bosses and elites only (`Mob.drawBar`). The guide arrow and caption (`entities/GuideArrow.ts`) show after ~1 s standing still or 4 s for a new target, and give way to name labels.
- **Threat pulses** (`ThreatPulses.ts`, maths in `threatPulse.ts`; core engine, cosmetic, local): at phone zoom the view is narrow, so a shooter's windup starts off-screen. Each such mob in windup (at most 3: soonest strike, then nearest) gets a soft pixel chevron on the screen edge toward it, in the one danger colour, closing and brightening toward the strike. Read from `Mob.shooterTell`, so **solo, host and guest all draw it and nothing is sent**. `keepClear` moves a pulse off the HUD, the item and interact buttons and, on touch, the stick, A and roll.
- **The quick-item button** (`ItemButton.ts`, storage `Hotbar.ts`, rules and thresholds in `systems/hotbar.ts`; core engine, per-player UI state): one 60 px
  button (up-left of A on touch, a corner on desktop, hidden for the DM) replaces the four slots. Tap fires on release (squashes at once) and uses the
  selected consumable; a swipe left or right cycles and never uses, skipping empty slots and items no longer carried. Keys 1-3, Q/E, F and the wheel do the same on a PC. Four slots stay stored, three are shown, only items with `use` qualify. A refusal (empty, or no need at full
  health) shakes the button with a `deny` thud and no text; a pop-up over the hero on cycle or use (`core/itemPopup.ts`). A use is an
  ordinary Inventory op through `pack.localOp`. **Co-op**: no protocol change: the guest applies it and asks the host like
  any menu action; selection and the pop-up setting stay local.
- **Interact marks** (`InteractMarks.ts`, maths `interactThings.ts`; core engine, cosmetic, local, nothing sent): a pooled pixel glint over each chest, person or pickup within 2 tiles (at most 8, brighter as the hero nears), and the touch `#btn-use` (hidden unless something is in reach, a code-drawn glyph of a bubble, hand or chest, no text). Fed by `Interactions.updateMarks`.
- **Joining closes the menu and reports through `#join-status`** (`JoinStatus.ts`, words from `net/joinStatus.ts`): connecting, waiting for the host, joined (fades), or a failure with its reason (stays until dismissed, brings the Online tab back to retry). Guest-only. The relay cannot tell a full game from a closed one.
- **The level-up badge is local** (`LevelSplash`; core engine): a small "Level N" chip for ~1.3 s, never over the fight, when the local hero's level rises (the host's `pack` for a guest); nothing is sent. The ring, glow and chime (`levelRing`) are the world's.
- **Voice input for the chat lines** (`dictation.ts`; core engine, local, co-op needs nothing): Enter on an empty DM-bar or request line (or Enter from the game, which opens it) starts the browser's SpeechRecognition and fills the line as words are heard; Enter again stops and submits. Browsers without recognition behave as before; the mic starts only on the player's own Enter and hushes the DM's voice (`voice.hush`) while it listens.
