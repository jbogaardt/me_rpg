# The Dungeon Master (co-op host) and its controls

Read this before touching the DM bar, director, cards, quests the DM gives, epics, possession, revive or narration (`src/scenes/overworld/Dm*.ts`, `src/systems/dm*.ts`, `src/hud/Dm*.ts`). Detail moved out of the root `CLAUDE.md` (which has the layers, commands, procedure and conventions); words such as "above" or "below" inside these bullets refer to this file or the map in the root. Keep the bullet for a feature up to date in the same change.

- **The Dungeon Master's controls (core engine, co-op): a bar that is always there and cards at the right moment, never an admin panel.** The DM has no menu tabs of their own but Party (`hud/Party.ts`, each player's level, HP, area and a **Go** button). What they steer comes two ways, both for whoever runs the world (the online DM; a solo player in a live world gets the cards only). **The DM bar** (`hud/DmBar.ts`, `#dm-bar`, along the bottom, online DM only, folded until the handle is tapped): a line to **Speak** (`DmDesk.say`, `cleanSay`, up to `MAX_SAY` 120 letters; `DmMsg.say` = [id, words] for `SAY_SHOW` 8 s, and every screen shows it in `#dm-speech`, the DM's too), **Stop time** (`T`, `FIXED_KEYS.stop`, `stopPressed()`, reserved like the menu and map keys; `Arena.timeStopped`: in `OverworldScene.updateSimulation` **the party can still walk and take doors**, but foes, crops, weather, the ground's clock, the training waves and what players do besides move (guests' actions, combos) stand still; the same key or button starts it again; the `dm` message carries `hold: 1` and every screen shows `#dm-hold`), **Smite** (`scenes/overworld/DmPowers.ts`, the plan in `systems/dmPowers.ts` `smitePlan`, tested: a storm of lightning over about 2.4 s, a few show bolts and one on every living foe in the DM's zone, which dies with no XP, gold or quest credit; `Simulation.lightning(strike, harmless)` so the bolts never hurt the party), **Freeze** (every foe in the DM's zone held still `FREEZE_TIME` 10 s, `Mob.freeze`), **Reset zone** (every foe there, the fallen too, `Mob.reset()` back home at full health; chests and the ground stay), and **Story / Sky / Pressure**, which open the cards below on demand (Story only in a live world). Smite, Freeze, Reset and the lightning are ordinary events and mob states, so friends see them through the usual snapshots. **The DM never spawns anything**: they shape how hard the world pushes (Pressure) and what happens in it (the story, the sky), never what is in it; the free-text person form and the Cast tab are gone (the log's `cast`/`npc` entries stay in `world.ts` as the door for AI help later). **The director and its cards** (`systems/dmDirector.ts`, pure and tested, says WHEN; `scenes/overworld/DmPrompts.ts` builds each card and does the pick; `hud/DmCard.ts`, `#dm-card`, draws it): the DM steers by **reacting**, not by digging: a card appears on its own with two or three buttons and the game carries on if it is ignored. The director watches the party's health, how far the DM's task has got and how long it has been quiet: **struggling** (anyone under `STRUGGLE_AT` 35% health, or a quest half done with the party under half): *Ease off* (`DmPowers.easeOff`: gentle pressure and a third of everyone's health back) or *Stay the course*; **too easy** (a quest `MIDWAY` 40% done, the party at 80% or better): *Turn up the heat* (`DmPowers.heat`: fierce pressure and the zone's idle foes called towards the party) or *Stay the course*; **a twist** (a quest half done, the party neither; a one-step quest counts as half done after `HALF_TIME` 75 s): two of *a storm rolls in* (or a blizzard) / *Night falls* / *Foes close in*, rotating; **which quest?** (nothing asked of the party for `IDLE_BEFORE_NEXT` 20 s, and 12 s after a quest ends): the land's quests (above); **lost** (a quest with no progress for `LOST_AFTER` 90 s and no hint yet, then again only after 120 s): *Point the way* or *Let them wander*; **that went quick / that was hard-won** (a quest just ended: quick and unhurt, `classify` smooth, or someone fell or dropped under 30% health, rough; a plain finish asks nothing): *Raise the stakes* (`heat`) or *Give them a breather* (`easeOff`), or *Keep the pace*; **a new land**: Clear skies / the land's weather / Night falls; **a fallen player**: raise them, bring them to me, or let them wake; **the story**: the party has finished what was told and there is a road (the cues, plus *Ask the party* with two or more; `DmDesk.openVote`, tallies on the buttons, `#vote` for friends). Urgent cards (fallen, story) wait `URGENT_GAP` 3 s after the last card, the rest `CARD_GAP` 10 s; a dismissed card is a breather (`SNOOZE` 25 s). A card has a timer (`CARD_TIME` 20 s, a fallen player 30 s; the story waits) and goes away by itself, by its ✕, by **Esc**, or by **a click or tap on the game** (`DmCard.dismiss`: nobody hunts for a close button); picking does the thing and closes it. The bar's Story / Sky / Pressure / Call a task / Raise buttons open the same cards on demand. The party's health, `Arena.pressure` and the task come from the host's state; the DM's words for the moves (`desk.say`: "Something stirs in the dark…", "A moment of calm.", "They are coming.") are pre-canned. **The big tools are hot buttons** (`#dm-hot`, `DmBar`: Smite, Freeze, Stop time, Reset zone, always on screen for the DM and, on a phone, a 2x2 grid above the button cluster; `body.dm-ui` hides the Inventory and Attack on-screen buttons, which do nothing for a DM, Attack coming back while a foe is held; the director never suggests them, only the nudges and Raise when someone falls): the game is the DM gently steering direction and intensity from how the party reacts, not driving it. **Nothing pops up for the DM: suggestions wait in an inbox** (`systems/dmRequests.ts` `Inbox`, pure and tested; `DmPrompts.waiting`/`openInbox`): the director's nudges and the party's requests both land there (one of a kind at a time, the director quiet while something waits, a nudge expiring after 90 s, a request after 180 s), shown as a pulsing **💬 Request(s)** hot button with a count that opens the oldest as an ordinary card; the bar's own buttons still open any card on demand. **Players ask the DM** with the 💬 button by the menu button (friends only, `hud/RequestPanel.ts`, `#req-btn`, `#request`): seven canned requests (`REQUESTS`: We are lost, This is too hard, This is too easy, Give us a quest, Give us an epic quest, Change the weather, Let us move on), each mapped to the card it brings up, **and a line to type their own words** (`MESSAGE_CODE`, up to `MAX_ASK` 120; each message waits in the inbox on its own); **there is no waiting between asks**. It rides in every input as state (`InputMsg.rq` = [counter, which, words?], protocol v39/v40, the host reads `DmDesk.requestsHeard` and keeps the last counter per friend). **The DM answers back, privately**: a card opened from a request offers three canned replies (`REPLIES`, the card stays up: answering is not deciding), a message card the same plus *Give them a quest*, and the bar's **Reply** button sends the text box to whoever asked (`DmPrompts.reply`, `DmDesk.replyTo`); it reaches those seats alone as `DmMsg.reply` = [id, words] (protocol v40) and shows in `#dm-speech` like the DM's other words. **No pop-ups while a foe is held** (`body.dm-possessing` hides the DM's speech, cards and the area banner; the director waits; the DM's words are not shown to them), and a possessed DM never takes a door of their own (`Zones.checkDoors` skips a hidden player; the Party tab's **Go** lets go first). **The DM's words are dismissible** (`#dm-speech` has a ✕, and Esc or a click on the game also sends them away) and may run to `MAX_SAY` 240 letters, shown `sayTime` long. **Online only the DM's quests count**: the story chain's givers only pass the time of day (`talk(hero, id, false)`), no `!`/`?` markers, no story arrow or tracker line, no story sites (solo only, `arena.role === 'solo'`), and the Quests tab shows the DM's quest (`dmQuestPage`). **A DM quest is told like an NPC's**: when one starts every player is read three pages in a dialog (`systems/dmBrief.ts` `briefing`, built on each screen from the quest on the wire and the names it knows, nothing more sent); the DM can **Cancel quest** and write **new instructions** (the bar's text box, "As instructions": `TaskView.note`, the twelfth value of `TaskWire`, up to `MAX_NOTE` 240, shown to the party in place of the usual telling; protocol v38). **Epic quests** (`systems/dmEpics.ts` the sagas and their gauge, pure and tested; `scenes/overworld/DmEpics.ts` on the host; the bar's **Epic quest** button, a request, or the director's inbox open the *Choose an epic quest* card): a saga is a chain of the DM's ordinary quests (`DmTasks`) across several lands, nine of them (Quiet Errands, The Long Road, The Great Hunt, A Harvest Against the Dark, The Lost Relics, Pilgrimage to the Far Shrine, The Siege of the Frontier, Beyond the Edge of the Map, The Hollow Crown), each a template of steps (a kind of quest, how many lands out, the DM's telling of it) with no content id in it. `planEpic` puts it onto the world being played by walking `neighbours` (`ZONE_LINKS`) from the DM's land through open country (`ZoneGraph.open`, a biome with a climate; never a shop or room), each step set the farthest land at its hop that the world has, so a two-land campaign tells a saga too. **Each saga carries a gauge** (`gauge`, `gaugeText` on the card's button: stars 1 to 5 for how hard (the template's, +1 for four or more lands), Short/Medium/Long/Grand and about how many minutes, the steps, the lands). `DmEpics.update` starts each step when the last is cleared away: it loads the land if nobody has been there (`Zones.ensure`), writes the quest from that land (`DmTasks.landOf`, a kind the land lacks falling back to one it has) and gives it with the saga's words as its note (`DmTasks.start`), so every player is read the telling and the tracker says `The Long Road 2/5: ...` (`TaskView.epic`, the thirteenth value of `TaskWire`, protocol v40). A step set in another land points the party at the door that leads there (`doorToward`, `pointTheWay`). A failed step, or the DM calling a step off (`DmTasks.onCancel`), ends the saga; finishing pays every player on top of the steps (`epicReward`), the DM says the saga's ending, and in a live world the story's next road is put in the inbox (`DmPrompts.suggest('story')`). **There are no cooldowns**: a DM is never made to wait (`SMITE_COOLDOWN`... are 0; a storm in flight is all a second Smite waits for). **The bar is folded by default** behind its `DM tools` handle (`DmBar.open`), and a click on the game folds it again; the menu closes on a click on its backdrop as well as Esc. A card redraws its buttons only when they change (a button replaced under a tap swallows it). `DmDesk.dialsView`/`setDial` (the capability dials, `DIALS`) have no screen any more; they stay as the engine's hook. **Quests the DM gives** (`systems/dmTasks.ts` the rules and `systems/dmQuests.ts` the offers, pure and tested; `scenes/overworld/DmTasks.ts` on the host): real quests, written from the land the DM stands in and left to the party to work out, one at a time, beside the story's chain (the chain is the spine, these are the DM's tool; the bar's **Quest** button, and the director's "Which quest?" card, offer three of different kinds, rotating): **Hunt N foes** (`what` = a foe kind alive in the land, 5 of a weak kind, 3 of a tough one; the party's kills of it in that zone, `Simulation.onKill(zone, key)`), **Gather N plants or stones** (a site kind the zone has two or more of; a touch counts once for the whole party, `DmTasks.touchSite`, `bits`), **Reach a place** (a named `MapData.places` entry; anyone within `VISIT_REACH` 6 cells) and **Find a relic** (a gear item chosen to fit the party's level, `relicsFor`; a glowing `relic` site, `RELIC_KIND`/`RELIC_LOOK`, hidden at a random open spot 12 to 45 cells from the party, created on every page from the quest's x,y by `OverworldScene.syncRelic`, `ZoneState.addSite`; whoever touches it keeps the item, `collect`). Nothing points at the goal: the map shows only a **search area** (`searchMiddle`, `SEARCH_RADIUS`; the quarry's area follows the nearest living foe or untouched site, retargeted every 2 s) and the tracker says what was asked with the count; the DM can **point the way** (`TaskBook.giveHint`: the quest arrow and a pin appear for everyone). Each pays every player XP and gold by its size (`taskReward`), is announced in the DM's voice (`DmDesk.say`) and stays on screen `TASK_LINGER` 5 s; a quest nobody finishes ends after `TASK_TIMEOUT` 25 min. The quest remembers how it went (`TaskResult`: how long against its `par`, the party's lowest health, who fell) for the director. Everyone sees it through `Arena.task` (`TaskView`: title, what, bits, hint; `DmMsg.task` is `TaskWire`, protocol v37). **Quests switch capabilities**: `Quest.capabilities` (quests.json; only the dials, `DIALS`, each true or false) applies to the whole party while a hero has that quest taken and not handed in (`questDials`, the first hero to say a thing wins; the DM's own dials win over it, `ContentManager.setQuestDials`/`effectiveDials`; host and solo work it out in `DmDesk.questSwitches`, a friend is told through the `dials` of the `dm` message): a hide-and-seek quest turns the map and guide off, a harvest turns farming on, a famine turns food and water on; a quest that switches food/water or farming on makes the goods it names fair (`checkAgainstCapabilities`); examples in the packs (`trace` guide off, `stones` and `road` needs on). **Narration** (`systems/narration.ts` `Narrator`, `scenes/overworld/DmNarration.ts`): pre-canned lines, quests.json `welcome` (an arc's `welcome` in the packs, quoting `{keeper}`/`{r1}`) said in the DM's voice as the host taps Start Game (`DEFAULT_WELCOME` when a story has none), then a chapter's `narration` the first time the party reaches it, one line at a time (`readTime`). **Revive** (`scenes/overworld/DmRevive.ts`): a defeat still wakes the player at the start of the world, whole (`Simulation.onDefeat` records where they fell for `REVIVE_WINDOW` 90 s); the DM gets a card (and a Raise button) to **raise them where they fell**, **bring them to me** (`Zones.travel`, so a friend's page follows) or leave it. **Possession** (`scenes/overworld/DmPossession.ts`; the DM is host only): click a living foe (`clickedAt`) and it is theirs: their move keys and attack drive it (`Mob.setDrive`, `Drive`: it walks at its own speed and strikes with its own windup, a shooter shoots along the way they aim, a ray kind fights close; its blows go through the usual damage rules, pressure included); Esc (or Tab) lets go instead of opening the menu; if the foe dies (the party can kill it and gets the usual XP) the DM steps out where it fell, and the DM can never die as they are not a target. While possessing, the DM's own body rides invisibly on the foe so the camera, zone and friends' view follow (`Player.hidden`, `FLAG.hidden` = 64 in a snapshot's player flags, so a friend does not see the DM's figure on top of the foe, protocol v36). `Mob.drive` is not unit-tested (it needs a Pixi `Character`); `DmPossession` is. **On a phone** (every DM action works by touch): the scene sets `body.touch-ui` while the on-screen controls are active, and the DM's bar then moves to the top (the pad and buttons own the bottom), is folded like on a desktop (`DmBar.open`; a folded bar still shows the hint and **Let go** while a foe is held) and never overlaps `#dpad` or `#buttons` (the e2e checks the rectangles); cards sit near the top with thumb-sized buttons (at least 44 px); **tap a foe** to possess it (`input.tap()`, any canvas touch; `clickedAt()` is a click or a tap, and a finger's reach is `FINGER` 1.7 times a click's); the pad moves the held foe and A strikes it; **Let go** (the bar's button, and ☰, which presses Esc) replaces Esc; Stop time is the bar's button and says so (no `[T]`, "Tap Resume time"). **`core/touch.ts` cancels every touch's default action outside an allow-list of screens (`keepDefault`), which also stops the browser making the click: a new tappable overlay must be added to that list (`#dm-bar`, `#dm-card` and `#vote` are).** **Co-op**: the host sends each friend a `dm` message (`DmMsg`: `shift`, `dials`, `vote`, optional `say`, `hold` and `task`) when it changes and once a second; a friend's vote rides in every input (`InputMsg.vt` = [vote id, choice]), so it is state, never lost; protocol v31, `say` v33, `hold` v34, `task` v35 (a real quest, v37). Leaving online play resets the clock, dials, vote, stopped time, task, narration, fallen list, possession and the prompter (`DmDesk.reset`, `DmPowers.reset`, `DmTasks.reset`, `DmNarration.reset`, `DmRevive.reset`, `DmPossession.reset`, `DmPrompts.reset`).
- **The Dungeon Master (co-op; core engine)** — there is one way to host, and **the host is the game's Dungeon Master**: from the moment a friend connects, player 0 (the host's local `Player`) has `dm` set (`Arena.setDm`, from `HostLink.enter`; `leave` gives them back their own hero, untouched). A guest knows player 0 is the DM with nothing on the wire (`DM_SLOT` in `systems/looks.ts`; `Arena.addRemote` sets it). The DM is seen in the world (a robed figure, `DM_LOOK`, gold ring and "DM" tag, `Arena.outfitOf`/`applyTags`) but is not one of the party: **`Arena.party()`** (the zone's players without the DM) is what the rules ask, and `players()` stays everyone for drawing, snapshots and the map. So no mob hunts or hurts them (`syncHeroBodies` leaves them out of `heroBodies`; mob shots, rays, lightning and `hurtHero` look in `party()`), a kill's XP, gold and quest credit go to the party only (`hitMob`), Mend and Ward pass them by (`Casting.friends`), they block nobody (`bodies()`), and they neither trade (`Trading`'s book only finds the party; the Trade tab hides them and is hidden for them) nor cast (`Simulation.combo`). `Simulation.updateDm` walks them (walls only, no ice, `DM_SPEED`; no meters run down) and their action key only reads signs and the lectern and advances dialogs (`handleAction`; `act` refuses). Their screens: the HUD shows a "Dungeon Master" badge instead of bars, combos and the tracker (`Hud.update(..., dm)`, `#hud-dm`); no quest arrow or harvest/drink buttons; the menu (`Menu.setOnline(online, dm)`) hides Status, Inventory, Magic, Quests and Trade and shows the **Party tab** (`hud/Party.ts`, `#menu-party`: each player's level, HP, area and quest, built with textContent, and a **Go** button, `OverworldScene.dmGoTo`, that takes the DM through the iris to that player). The Online tab names the host "Dungeon Master" (roster, "Connected to the Dungeon Master (host)"), and so does the map legend. Protocol v29 (what player 0 means). Solo has no DM.
