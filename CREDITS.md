# Credits

## Tilesets (`public/tilesets/`)

Overworld tilesets "Avalon / Grassland" by Len Pabin (inspired by Zyndikate), 2009. The sheets carry a
GPL license stamp in their corner (GPL3 on most, GPL2 on the water sheet). Verify the exact license at the
original source before redistributing, and keep this notice with the art.

`ground_plus.png`, `frost_ground.png`, `frost_objects.png` and `dry_objects.png` are recoloured derivatives of
those sheets (snow, ice, frosted and withered trees), generated by `tools/map/recolor.ts` (`npm run tilesets`),
so the same license applies.

## Terrain atlas (`public/tilesets/lpc_terrain.png`, source in `art/lpc_terrain/`)

"Tiled Terrains" (LPC terrain atlas) submitted by adrix89, https://opengameart.org/content/tiled-terrains, licensed
[CC-BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) or [GPL 3.0](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html).
It collects Liberated Pixel Cup art by Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), Matthew Nash, Nushio, William.Thompsonj, adrix89,
Casper Nilsson, Daniel Eddeland, Johann Charlot, Skyler Robert Colladay and others; the full list is in
[`art/lpc_terrain/Attribution.txt`](art/lpc_terrain/Attribution.txt), kept with the art. The atlas is used unmodified:
`tools/map/terrain.ts` reads its Tiled tileset (`Terrain.tsx`) to autotile the ground, and some props are cut from it.

`jungle_objects.png`, `dead_objects.png` and `swamp_objects.png` are further recolours of the Avalon trees, made by
`tools/map/recolor.ts` like the frost and dry ones above.

## Shop buildings (`public/tilesets/town.png`, source in `art/town_rpg_pack/`)

"Town RPG Pack" by Luis Zuno ([@ansimuz](https://ansimuz.com)), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
(public domain; credit is not required but is given). The inn and the tall house are cut out of the pack's tile map,
scaled 2x, recoloured and given doors and emblems by `tools/map/town.ts`.

## Characters (`public/sprites/lpc/`, catalog in `sprites/parts.ts`)

Hero, NPC and monster art comes from the [Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator)
and the Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) art it collects (https://lpc.opengameart.org). `npm run sprites` recolours the
generator's layers with its palettes and repacks them onto one sheet layout (`src/sprites/doll.ts`), so these files are
**modified adaptations**; the game then stacks the layers at runtime.

The art is under several open licences, chosen per asset by its authors: CC-BY-SA 3.0, CC-BY 3.0/4.0, OGA-BY 3.0,
GPL 2.0/3.0 and CC0. The most restrictive, **CC-BY-SA 3.0, requires crediting the authors and releasing adaptations
(the packed sheets in `public/sprites/lpc/`) under the same or a later CC-BY-SA, or the GPL**. Keep these notices with
the art, and do not wrap it in DRM.

**Per-asset credits** (authors, licences and source links of every file in use) are generated into
[`public/sprites/lpc/credits.csv`](public/sprites/lpc/credits.csv) on each `npm run sprites -- build`, and are also
served with the game at `sprites/lpc/credits.csv` and linked from the pause menu.

## UI (`src/ui/`, source in `art/rpg_gui_v1/`)

"RPG GUI construction kit v1.0" by Lamoot, licensed [CC-BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
(https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-gui-construction-kit-v10). Modified: pieces cut from the sheet, the
loose frame corners and rails stitched into closed frames (`tools/ui/cut-gui.ts`), and the wood and paper
backgrounds re-encoded as JPEG. Bar colours are sampled from the kit's gradients.
