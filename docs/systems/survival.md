# Food and water, crops, weather, day and night

Read this before touching needs, ponds, crops, weather or the day clock. Keep it up to date in the same change ("above"/"below" in the text mean this file or the root map).

- **Food and water** — `systems/needs.ts` (Pixi-free, tested): `Hero.needs` = `{ food, water }` (0..100, start full). They drain with time (food lasts about 20 minutes, water 14; `drainNeeds`, run in `Simulation.updatePlayer`, so solo/host only) and are refilled by supplies whose `use` has `food`/`water` (`bread`, `roastMeat`, `apple`, `waterskin`, sold by the apothecary, eaten from the Inventory like potions; `useFromPack` refuses when nothing would change). `needsRegen` scales the natural HP/MP regeneration by the worse meter (well fed 1.25x, hungry 0.5x, empty 0; gear that mends is unaffected) and only when both meters are empty does it cost health (`starveRate`: 3% of max HP per second, in `Simulation.updatePlayer`; it leaves 1 HP, never kills). `Hud` shows Food and Water bars in a second row under HP/MP (`#hud-needs`). **Drinking from a pond**: standing at the edge of clear pond water (surface `water`; the sea, swamp `bog` and ice do not count) while not fully watered shows a **Drink** button by the water (none by an empty pond) (`drinkableNear`, `DRINK_REACH`, `thirsty`; drawn by `hud/HarvestButtons.ts` with `DRINK_KEY`; it reads **Stop** while drinking). **The action key (A on touch, Space or a left click) drinks too**: in `OverworldScene.act`, after chests, crops and sites and before picking up or swinging, `Simulation.pondAction` stops a player who is drinking or starts one for whom `canDrink` (thirsty, beside a pond that is not empty), so by a pond A drinks instead of attacking while thirsty, **but only while no enemy is near** (`foeNear`: a live mob within `FOE_NEAR`, 6 cells): in a fight A always attacks and stops a drink in progress (the Drink button still drinks); a guest's A press reaches the host's `act` like any other, and the guest flips its own `guestDrinking` to keep the button label right. Drinking is steady, not in sips: `Simulation.startDrinking` turns the hero to the pond and sets `Player.drinking`, then each tick (`drinkTick`) `POND_FLOW` (8) water a second flows from the pond into them (`sipWater`) until they are full, walk off, press Stop or the pond is dry ("The pond is dry"); the total floats when they stop. **Ponds drain**: each pond (`pondId`, a flood fill of joined water cells) holds `WATER_PER_CELL` (1.5) water per cell (`pondCapacity`) and `PondLevels` (one per `ZoneState` as `ponds`) keeps how far each is drained (0 full .. 1 dry). **Only rain fills a pond again**: `Simulation.weather` calls `ponds.rain(rainFill(precip, intensity), dt)`; rain fills a dry pond in `RAIN_FILL_TIME` (120 s) at full intensity, a storm twice as fast, snow, sand, ash and clear skies not at all, and only in zones someone is in (where weather runs). A pond drunk dry is `refilling` (`PondLevels.refilling`) until full again and fills **from the middle out**: `PondBeds.drawRefill` cuts the water back into the bed ring by ring from the middle while the outer rings stay dry, and lily pads return as the water reaches them; a pond only part drunk goes back the way it went. Levels (and the refilling flag) are kept in `ZoneMemory.ponds` while a zone is unloaded. **Drawn in layers**: `pondLayers` splits a pond into rings from the shore in, and `drainedLayers` says how many are gone and how far the next has faded; `entities/PondBeds.ts` (a `Graphics` in the zone's `world.layer` at ground level, blurred soft, cached as a texture and redrawn only when a level visibly changes) washes each gone layer in the ponds' own blue a shade lighter (shallow water, kept off the sandy bank) and fades the one going now to it, so the deep blue shrinks ring by ring; only as the last layer goes does the earthen bed fade in, so a pond is brown only when empty. The bed follows the shore: `map/outline.ts` (`cellsOutline`, Pixi-free, tested) traces a set of cells the way the corner-painted terrain draws it (marching squares over the cell corners, the edge `BED_REACH` of the way into the bank cells, rounded by Chaikin's `roundOff`), so it covers the water at the edges and corners; its edge is the banks' tan, so the ground runs on into it, and each ring further in is the same smooth shape a size smaller and a shade darker. Props standing in a pond's water (lily pads) fade out with the last of it and come back as rain fills it (`MapView.setObjectAlpha`: a fade per map object, kept while its chunk is freed and rebuilt). **Co-op**: the host owns the meters and sends them whole in `PackMsg.needs` (protocol v20); a guest only displays them. Its Drink/Stop button sends `DoMsg` op `drink` (item 1 start, 0 stop; protocol v27), which the host checks against the pond beside that friend (`PackSync.tradeFor`); a guest's own idea of whether it is drinking (`guestDrinking`) ends when it walks or is full, as the host's does. The host alone drains and rains on ponds and sends each zone's drained ponds in the snapshot (`SnapMsg.ponds`: flat id, thousandths drained, negative while refilling; absent = all full; `pondsWire`/`pondsFromWire`); the guest only draws them. Not built yet: drops from enemies, foraging.
- **Crops (harvest and eat)** — `systems/crops.ts` (Pixi-free, tested): `CROPS` (potato, carrot, cabbage, tomato, strawberry, pumpkin, watermelon: each a `grow` time in seconds, a produce `item` and a `yield`), `cropStage` (four stages, the last is ripe) and `harvest(hero, kind)` (the produce goes to the pack, or, with no room for all of it, nothing is taken). The produce are items of the campaign's items.json with a `use` that fills food and water, eaten from the Inventory like any supply (`iconKind` gives them the `food` icon). Art: `public/tilesets/crops.png` (a row per crop in `CROP_KINDS` order, a column per stage, 32x64 frames), cut from the CC-BY-SA "[LPC] Crops" sheet in `art/lpc_crops/` by `tools/map/crops.ts` (part of `npm run tilesets`; `SOURCE` says where each crop is on the sheet). Zones place plots with `z.crop(kind, col, row)` (solid cell, `MapData.crops`, checked by `tools/map/check.ts`: known kind, reachable from beside, at most `MAX_CROPS` = 30 per map; the Greenwood has a farm by the crossroads). **Watering**: a plot grows in `CROP_PHASES` (3) phases, each needing its own watering: a picked plot is dry (`Crop.thirsty`) and floats "Needs water" (a `BitmapText` above it) until a player presses the action key beside it with an item that `waters` (a Waterskin) in their pack (`waterWith` uses one up; none: "Need a Waterskin"; the apothecary sells them); watered, it grows for a third of `grow` (`phaseTime`), shows the next stage and dries out again, and the third watering carries it to ripe. `entities/Crop.ts` draws one (`phase`, `progress` within it, `wet`; `grow(dt)` advances a watered one, `pick()` restarts it dry, `water()`, `remember`/`restore` for the zone memory); a ripe plot shows a **Harvest button** by the plant (`hud/HarvestButtons.ts`: HTML in `#harvest-layer`, placed each frame from the plot's world position, so a press never also swings at the canvas; shown only for plots within `CROP_REACH` of the local player and not while a menu, dialog or door is up): pressing it picks the plot (`harvestPressed`: host/solo pick directly, a guest sends an action press that the host's `act` turns into the pick); in `OverworldScene.act` the action key also picks a ripe plot (and says "Pack full" when it cannot), else waters a dry one (after chests, before sites). Solo and the host grow the plots of every occupied zone (the per-zone loop in `updateSimulation`); an unloaded zone keeps each plot's age in `ZoneMemory.crops` (phase, progress and watered; time away does not count). **Co-op**: the host decides and picks (a guest's action press is handled like a chest's, the produce arrives through `pack`); each plot's state rides in the snapshot as a small code (`SnapMsg.crops`: one number per plot, `cropCode`/`cropFromCode`: the phase and whether it is dry, or ripe) and a guest only keeps its own timer for how far along a watered phase is (`Crop.grow(dt, false)`, never ending a phase on its own, `setCode`). More farms: place plots with `z.crop` and paint the ground with `z.paint('dirt', ...)`; more crops: a row on the sheet (`SOURCE`), an entry in `CROPS`, an item.
- **Weather** — each biome has its own (`systems/weather.ts`, Pixi-free, tested): a zone's `MapData.climate` (a key of `CLIMATES`, set by `defineBiome({ climate })`; rooms have none) says which precipitation can fall there: `temperate` (greenwood, eastwood, town: rain, storm), `coast` (beach: rain, storm, windier), `desert` (sandstorms only), `jungle` (heavy rain and storms), `swamp` (wetlands, marsh: rain), `undead` (ash, storms) and `frost` (snow, blizzards; snow falls nowhere else). A `Weather` (one per `ZoneState`) keeps **precipitation** (`Precip`: rain, storm, snow, blizzard, sand, ash, or none) and **wind** (`windLevel`: none, a breeze or a gale, plus the precipitation's own `LEAN`; direction ±1) separately: `CLEAR_SHARE` (80%) of the time there is neither; otherwise it is precipitation alone, wind alone, or both. Spells last 90-180 s, clear or not, so the time share matches, and ease in and out over 6 s. Only zones with a player in them are advanced (`Simulation.weather`, in the per-zone loop; a zone that is unloaded starts afresh, and a new `Weather` starts at a random point of a spell), and lightning is rolled there too (`Weather.strikeDue`, only in a built-up storm; the bolt lands around a random player *in that zone*: a `lightning` world event, and a player within `LIGHTNING_RADIUS` takes 35% of their max HP). `entities/WeatherLayer.ts` only draws a `Weather` (a haze for some, a pooled field of streaks/flakes/grains/ash in one `ParticleContainer`, gust streaks for wind alone, a flash and a seeded jagged bolt); the layer is part of its zone, so in a door transition each biome's sky is pushed in with it. Wind blows leaves (`Ambient.wind`). Weather is a backdrop and must stay cheap: no full-screen frame or heavy dimming (a full-screen haze layer is the costliest part; snow has none), and drops are placed by a low-discrepancy sequence (`Drop.u/v`) so they spread evenly instead of clumping. **Co-op**: the host rolls every occupied zone's weather and sends each friend *their zone's* in the snapshot (`SnapMsg.w`: `[precip index + 1 or 0, wind %, intensity %, 1 if it blows right]`; `Weather.snapshot`/`setRemote`); a guest never rolls its own. Dev/playtest: `scenes.current.setWeather('rain', 0.85)` forces weather in the zone you are in (`setWeather(null, 0.35)` is a breeze alone).
- **Day and night** — `systems/daynight.ts` (Pixi-free, tested): the time of day is 0..1 (0 midnight, 0.5 noon), `DAY_LENGTH` (12 real minutes) per day, and `skyColor(time)` interpolates the `SKY` stops (just day, night and a short blend between) into a multiply tint. `DayClock` reads the **wall clock**, so every co-op player sees the same hour with nothing on the wire (no protocol change). `entities/NightLayer.ts` is one screen-sized sprite with `blendMode: 'multiply'`, in the scene root over the stage and under the quest arrow and door transition; it hides itself in rooms (`MapData.indoor`) and in full daylight. Purely cosmetic for now. Dev/playtest: `scenes.current.setHour(22)` jumps this client to an hour.
