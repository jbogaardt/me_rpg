# Art credits

These pictures are from the **Wyrmsun** game (https://andrettin.github.io/), taken from the OpenGameArt pack
"Wyrmsun CC0 - over 900 items" (https://opengameart.org/content/wyrmsun-cc0-over-900-items, redistributed by Drummyfish, licence **CC0**,
no credit required). The pack's own credits file names the authors: Jinn (towers, the goblin spearman and the veteran warrior) and
Exidelo (the goblin thief, the dwarvish mercenaries and the jarl). Please support them.

- Units are the factions' portrait icons: goblins (`goblin_*`), dwarves (`dwarf_axefighter`, `dwarf_thane`), Germanic (`germanic_*`).
- Towers are 64x64 frames cut from their sheets in the game. `dwarf_*` are the dwarves'. `wood_watch_tower`, `stone_guard_tower` and
  `catapult_tower` have names that several factions share in the flat pack, so which faction's version it holds is not known.
- `tools/towercraftArt.ts` copies them out of the pack and checks each against the pack's credits file (CC0, and a name listed once
  unless it is one of those shared towers). Only CC0 files are used: the pack's CC-BY-SA and GPL material, including Wyrmsun's animated
  unit sheets, is deliberately not.

## Ground

`ground.png` is eight tiles (four grass, four sand) cut from Me RPG's `public/tilesets/ground_tiles.png`: the "Avalon / Grassland"
overworld tileset by Len Pabin (inspired by Zyndikate), 2009, which carries a GPL stamp in its corner. It is the same art and the same
status as in Me RPG, so see the root `CREDITS.md` for its licence note and verify it at the source before relying on it.
