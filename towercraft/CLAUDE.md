# towercraft: a small real-time strategy game on roads between castles

A separate game on the same site (built into `dist/towercraft/`, see the root `CLAUDE.md`). It shares no game code with Me RPG; only
the conventions (Vite, strict TypeScript, Pixi-free rules with unit tests beside them, Prettier, ESLint) and, for online play, Me RPG's connection layer (see Online).

## The game

- **Duels and co-op** (title screen: Duel / 2 v 1 / 2 v 2; design and rules in `src/sim/CLAUDE.md`). A duel is one road between two castles, five maps (`greenwood` the classic and the sim's default, `frostlands` the menu's, `desert`, `mire`, `ember`); units walk it and meet in between. **Co-op**: two on a side against computers, on four maps whose roads fork between three or four camps (your partner is a computer, or a friend online). The map is `Match.board` (`board.ts`): the rules never read a map constant. Destroy the other castle (co-op: all of the other side's) to win, or after `TIME_LIMIT` have more castle health.
- **The UI shows, it does not tell**: only a see-through wood and stone overlay. The dock shows **only what you can do now** (units, buildings, upgrades whose castle level and building are in place). A thumbnail opens a *card* of pictures (`icons.ts`: bars against the best of any race) with the purchase button. Tap a tower on the field for its card.
- **Commanding is direct** (`main.ts`, input block): pick units by tap, **box** (left-drag; touch: press and hold), double-tap (all of the kind), Shift/Ctrl-tap, All or Ctrl+A; then tap (or right-click) a visible rival tower or castle to send the group (`setFocus`, `Unit.focus`: it ignores the rest until that tower falls; `canTarget` refuses the unreachable).
- **Two currencies, wood and stone** (`Cost`, `price()`; `Player.wood/stone`): everything costs both. The castle pays `CastleDef.income[level]` every `PAY_EVERY` (5 s); mills (wood) and quarries (stone) (`TowerDef.econ`, `limit` 3) add more; razing one cuts the foe's income. **Purchases are instant**; only money and `UNIT_CAP` (120) limit spam.
- **The castle sells only its base unit; producer buildings unlock the rest** (`UnitDef.needs`, `TowerDef.trains`, `unitUnlocked`, `towerUnlocked`). A building may need another (`TowerDef.needs`) and be limited (`limit`). Razing one locks its units again.
- **Attack styles** (`UnitDef`): `range` (long range hits across to another run of the road), `splash` (a blast or trample), `multi` (one blow strikes that many foes: a sweep or flame), `suicide` (dies on its first strike, no bounty), `siege` (damage x vs towers and castle), `hitsAir`, `cloak`. Flyers (`air`) **follow the road** over everything; only air-capable things hurt them.
- **Units leave the road to take a tower** (`detourFor`, `Unit.off`): out of reach from the road, a unit steps off at the road point straight across from the tower (square to the road there, never a slant, up to `Match.detourMax` cells, `DETOUR_MAX` in the game), fights, and returns when the tower falls or the order is `bypass`.
- **Orders** (`setOrder`): units `attack` or `bypass` towers and units, separately; the castle is always attacked once in reach.
- **Defence**: towers shoot units only. *Attackers* hit `ground`, `air` or `both` (`splash`, poison `dot`); *auras* `power` (at most double) or `heal`; *detectors* reveal cloaked units; **bunkers** (`TowerDef.bunker`) are loaded by `loadBunker` with ONE garrison-capable ranged soldier per slot for its share of the price; each shoots with its own stats, range+`rangeBonus` and upgrades, and dies with it. `damage: 0` never shoots.
- **Upgrades** (`UpgradeDef`): a click only arms the button ("Confirm?"), a second within 3 s buys (dock and castle; `confirmed` in `main.ts`); per race ground/air x damage/health, three levels of +15% (`boostOf`), bought at a smithy-type building (`buyUpgrade`, instant). Health applies to units bought later (`Unit.maxHp`), damage at once.
- **Build area and fog of war** (`inBuildArea`, `visionOf`, `canSee`, `isSeen`; in `src/sim/CLAUDE.md`): you build only on ground your castle or a tower sees, never within `FOE_CLEAR` of a foe's castle; castle upgrades ask for buildings (`upgradeLacks`); hidden enemies are not drawn, targeted or splashed.
- **Look** (`main.ts`, cosmetic): units at `SIZE` x2; walk, stand, strike (`Shot.by`), tower fx, pay-day figures. No sparkles.
- **Buildings grow with upgrades** (`trim.ts`, cosmetic, Pixi-free, drawn by `drawTrim`): a castle gets +8% size, two race-coloured pennants and, at the top level, a base ring; a smithy gets a pennant and +5% size per 3 upgrade levels its owner bought. Drawn from `Match` state on each client, so lockstep is untouched.
- **Hurt and falling buildings** (`wreck.ts`, cosmetic): smoke below half health, flames below 30%; a razed tower (`sync`'s `gone` hook keeps its sprite) or a castle at 0 shakes and sinks into the ground with dust and rubble that fades. The end screen waits for the castle (`finale`) and is see-through over the map.
- **Races** (`races.ts`): swarm/Goblins (fast, weak, in groups), titan/Dwarves (slow, strong), legion/Germanic (archers, sweepers, sappers, catapults): 4-6 ground and 2-3 air units each.

## Online

The title screen's **Solo / Host / Join** picks solo (the AI) or a game with a friend over Me RPG's relay and peer-to-peer link (a duel, or co-op as partners): lockstep, so `src/sim` must stay exact. Design and rules in `src/net/CLAUDE.md`.

## Code

- **Rules are Pixi-free and deterministic** (`src/sim/`, enforced by ESLint): `sim.ts` the match, `races.ts` the data, `board.ts` the maps, `ai.ts` the seeded opponent, `testkit.ts` test helpers. `main.ts` draws a `Match`, owns the camera and turns taps into commands.
- **Art** is Wyrmsun's, copied by `tools/towercraftArt.ts` from a clone of its repo (`public/art/CREDITS.md`): unit sheets CC-BY-SA 3.0, CC0 castles, towers, buildings. Every map is `GROW` (2) x the 36x55 grid the roads were drawn on (72x110 cells plus `EDGE` (8) open columns each side; `greenwood` 111 rows so its middle run sits on a row; `Board.cols/rows` drive the camera and fog) for room to build. **Distances are stretched too**: `races.ts` is written for the small grid; `RACES` has tower and castle ranges, reaches, sights, radii and unit speeds times `REACH_SCALE` (1.5); unit attack ranges are NOT stretched (balance). Unit tests play `DRAWN_RACES` on the drawn field (`testkit.ts`; `Match.races`/`detourMax`). Scenery is sparse (it blocks building). **Art loads lazily** (`main.ts`: `prefetch` on the title screen, `ensureAssets` behind a progress bar on pick). Fields (`art/maps/<id>.png`) come from `npm run towercraft:board [-- <id>]` (`tools/towercraftBoard.ts`), which also writes `src/sim/scenery.ts`; roads are `src/sim/road.ts`. **A new map** is a `ROADS` entry, a `THEMES` entry and a generator run; `board.test.ts` checks fairness. `greenwood` and the two-against-two fields are turned half way round onto themselves.

## State of play

**Lab and balance**: `npm run towercraft:lab -- --help` (`tools/towercraftLab.ts`: duel maps x races x `STRATEGIES`, win rates, CSV, `--set` what-ifs), `npm run towercraft:coop` (`tools/towercraftCoop.ts`: the co-op maps, and the lone side's `pace`) and `npm run towercraft:balance [-- N [seedBase]]`; re-run after changing `races.ts`, `ai.ts` or `sim.ts` constants. Pace: income x0.4 (`PACE`, per-race lever `INCOME_BALANCE`); games run about 15 min, from minute 18 castle blows grow (`suddenDeath`).

**The AI** (`Style` from its seed): mills and quarries first, tech, waves of at least `MIN_WAVE` units (bombs against towers, sweeps against crowds, archers against air), upgrades, loaded bunkers, castle levels, towers as far forward as they see road, bypass when it could kill the castle fast. It reads the foe only through `foeUnits`, `foeTowers`, `foeCastle`.

Next: a *beacon* tower; detector units; slow and armour; rally and attack-move; online leave button, rejoin, spectating.
