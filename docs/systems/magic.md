# Wands, tomes, combos and skills

Read this before touching spells, combos, skills, the trainer or training grounds. Keep it up to date in the same change ("above"/"below" in the text mean this file or the root map).

- **Wands, tomes and combos** — a wizard's shop (`shop_wizard`, door in the town, in the east beyond the ranger and clear of the north road) sells wands and tomes (`ITEMS`: `apprenticeWand`, `stormcallerWand`, `noviceTome`, `arcaneGrimoire`; `Item.focus`, a `Focus` from `systems/spells.ts`, Pixi-free and tested). They are weapons (main hand only; never auto-worn over a blade, see `isBetter`) whose plain attack is a free bolt (`Casting.basic`, as strong as the weapon's attack; a wand's cooldown is short, a tome's long) and which have **combos**: a string of direction taps (five on a first-tier weapon, six on a second-tier one, e.g. `urulr` = W D W A D, or the same flicked on the on-screen D-pad; never the same direction twice in a row) that casts a spell for MP, much stronger than the plain attack (damage/healing = `spellPower` = magic stat + the weapon's `focus.power`, times the combo's `power`). **Every wand and tome is unique**: no spell and no string is on two weapons (`spells.test.ts` checks it, and at least three combos per weapon, none hidden inside another or inside a skill's). Apprentice Wand: Arcane Bolt, Spark Fan, Blink. Stormcaller Wand (storm): Chain Lightning, Storm Lance (pierces a long line), Smite (one heavy strike), Gale (a cone that blows foes back). Novice Tome (support): Nova, Mend, Ward. Arcane Grimoire (arcane): Meteor, Soul Drain (heals you for half the damage), Stasis (freezes foes still, `Mob.freeze`) and Vortex (drags foes in). Costs run 6-22 MP. `core/combo.ts` (`ComboReader`, Pixi-free, tested) takes the move presses (`actionPressed` up/down/left/right, so rebinding and the pad work; nothing counts while a menu, dialog or door transition is up). It is forgiving, especially on touch: a tap of the direction just tapped is ignored (a wobbling thumb does not count twice), the pause allowed between taps is `COMBO_WINDOW_TOUCH` 1.3 s on the pad (0.7 s on a keyboard), and a thumb landing on a pad diagonal (two directions at once, touch only) counts for nothing and yields the finished combo's index into `weapon.focus.combos`, which rides in `Command.combo`. `scenes/overworld/Casting.ts` carries the spell out (solo/host): `bolt`, `fan`, `chain`, `dash` (Blink), `nova`, `blast` (Meteor), `heal`, `ward` (`Player.ward`, immune and tinted), `lance`, `smite`, `gale`, `drain`, `freeze`, `vortex`; a spell that can do nothing (no target, nobody hurt) costs no MP. `hud/Hud.ts` shows the weapon's combos in `#combo-hud`, lighting the arrows typed so far; the Inventory and shop show them too (`hud/itemText.ts`). **Co-op**: the guest detects the combo locally and sends `InputMsg.cb` (a running counter) and `ci` (the index) like an action press; the host runs it on that friend's own weapon (`HostLink.command`); effects ride as the usual `bolt`/`text`/`fx` events (new fx `castGlow`, `nova`, `blast`, `zap`, `heal`, `ward`, `blink`, `frost`; `castGlow` also tells a guest to play the cast pose) and `FLAG.ward` tints a warded player. **Skills (innate spells, the training wizard)** — `systems/skills.ts` (Pixi-free, tested): `SKILLS` are spells a hero carries (`Hero.skills` learned, `Hero.loadout` ready; not items, so they work with a sword, a bow or a wand), tapped like a combo (four directions, a full turn; never contained in any wand/tome combo, `skills.test.ts` checks) and weaker than a wand's spells. One new skill every five levels: **Arcane Edge** (level 1, 5 MP, `ruld`: the next 3 sword swings, arrows or wand bolts within 12 s add magic damage, `Simulation.takeEdge`, `Player.edge`), **Spark Volley** (level 5, 6 MP, `urdl`: a fan of three bolts, each at 0.7 of spell power, via `Casting.fan`; offensive, weaker than a wand's fan), **Iron Skin** (level 10, 8 MP, `dlur`: 8 s of taking 55% of damage, `Player.guard`, applied in `Simulation.harm`) and **Spirit Mark** (level 15, 9 MP, `ldru`: a pulse that stings foes within 4 cells and marks them for 8 s, every hit on them does 30% more, `Casting.marks`/`markFactor` in `Simulation.hitMob`); effects show as a tint (`auraTint`, `FLAG.ward/edge/guard` for guests). **Only two skills are ready at once** (`LOADOUT_SLOTS`; `learn` fills a free slot, `setLoadout` is the one validated way to change them): a wand or tome's own combos do not count toward the two, so a wand gives 2 skills plus all its combos. `heroCombos(hero, zone)` is the one list the combo reader, HUD and `Casting.combo` use (the wand/tome's combos first, then `activeSkills`; the index is what travels as `ci`); `activeSkills` puts **the skill being tested in slot 2 of its training ground** (`skillOfZone(zone)`, slot 1 stays), so it can be tried on the wave. The **Magic tab** of the menu (`hud/Magic.ts`, `#menu-magic`; the scene attaches `MagicHandlers` on `enter`) shows the two slots, what each skill does and its arrows, and buttons to ready/remove them any time (it also lists the weapon's combos). They are learned in **trials**: the trainer (`GIVERS.orin`, `z.giver('orin', ...)` in `shop_wizard`; the action key beside him reads `trainerTalk`, which explains the skill, the two slots, the Magic menu and that the skill is on loan in slot 2 during the trial; when the last line is dismissed the trial starts) sends the player through the iris to that skill's training ground (`maps/training_novice|apprentice|journeyman|adept.ts`, built by `tools/map/training.ts`: indoor zones, a doormat out at the bottom, enemies that do not come back, `Mob.respawns = false` for `isTrialZone`; the skill is the next unlearned one whose `minLevel` the hero has reached). `scenes/overworld/Training.ts` (host/solo) runs them: `start` (checks level and knowns, loads the zone, resets the wave, full HP/MP, `Player.trial`), `update` (every enemy down: each player in the zone being tested for it learns the skill, is told which slot it went to or how to swap, and is sent back after 3.5 s; walking out of the door gives up), `fail` (called from `Simulation.defeat`: a defeat in a trial wakes you in the trainer's room instead of the start of the world). The later trials' enemies (`chieftain`, `cultist` for level 10, `warlord`, `wraith` for level 15) are tuned for those levels (nothing in the open world hurts a level 10+ hero). `checkDoors` does not ask a trial zone for a door back. **Co-op**: a guest reads the trainer's lines locally (its hero's level, skills and loadout are synced) and asks the host with `DoMsg` op `train`; the host checks the trainer is beside them and starts the trial (the guest follows to the new zone like any door); the Magic tab's choices go as `DoMsg` op `loadout` (`item` = `loadoutCode`) and the host validates them; `PackMsg.skills` (`skillMask`) and `PackMsg.loadout` tell the guest what it knows and has ready. A **lectern** in the wizard's room (`systems/lectern.ts`, prop `lectern` placed by `maps/shop_wizard.ts`; the action key beside it opens a `Dialog` with a page per wand/tome listing every combo; local reading only, nothing to sync) shows all of them. Wands and tomes have no art on the hero yet (`look: {}`; the weapon vanishes in the cast pose anyway). The old right-click/K/B Time Bolt (`Hero.spell`) is gone.
