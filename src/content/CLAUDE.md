# src/content (data-driven worlds)

Keep it up to date in the same change ("above"/"below" in the text mean this file or the root map).

- **Content (data-driven: `public/content/<campaign>/` and `src/content/`)** — what the world holds is data, not engine code. A **campaign** is a folder of five JSON files (validated on load, `src/content/schema.ts`, Pixi-free and tested; a bad file stops the boot with every problem listed, `ContentError`): **`entities.json`** (every mob and NPC by id: `type` `mob`/`npc`, `name`, `notes` for designers, `sprite` = a paper-doll `Outfit`, `scale`; mobs have `stats` {hp, attack, defense, speed px/s, sight px, xp, gold} and `behavior` {windup, recover, stagger, `shot`, `ray`; colours `"#rrggbb"`}; an NPC placed by a scenario says its `lines`), **`biomes.json`** (by `biome_type`: its on-screen `name` (`ZONE_NAMES`), `tint` (the iris rim, `ZONE_TINTS`), `links` (the zones walkable from it in preference order: the guide's routes, `nextZone`; checked against the maps' doors), the `map` it is drawn from, `climate` (null in a room), `music` (unused: there is no audio yet), `tilesets` (written by `npm run map -- build` from the map; loaded alongside the map JSON) and `generation.roam`: enemies spread over the biome's open ground when the zone loads, deterministic by `seed`, `content/populate.ts` `roam`) **`quests.json`** (the story: `giver` (whose chain it is), `givers` (name, `look` = an npc entity, tag, zone), `sites` (gather spots: name, shape mushroom/stone/sand, colours, notes), `chapters`, `quests` (the chain in story order, the `Quest` shape of `systems/quests.ts`; objectives `kill`/`gather`/`bring`, rewards naming item ids of its items.json) and `epilogue`), **`items.json`** (its goods, below) and **`scenarios.json`** (a graph of nodes: `node_id`, `biome_type`, optional arrival `spawn`, `entities` [{ entity, x, y } in the map's cells] and `choices` [{ text, next, requires?: { stat: level/attack/defense/magic, min } or { skill } }]; `start` is where the game begins). `ContentManager` (`src/content/ContentManager.ts`) fetches them from a `ContentSource` (one method, `fetch(file)`; the static files are `assets.ts` `staticContent`, `UrlContentSource` reads any URL serving the same JSON, so a database is a new source and one line in `main.ts`), caches them and answers `getScenario`, `getBiomeInfo`, `getEntityBaseStats`, `mobKind`, `populate`; `install()` fills the registries the rules read by id (`MOB_KINDS` in `systems/combat.ts`, `NPC_LOOKS` in `systems/looks.ts`, `ZONE_NAMES`/`ZONE_LINKS`/`ZONE_TINTS` in `systems/places.ts`, `QUESTS`/`CHAPTERS`/`GIVERS`/`SITE_KINDS`/`QUEST_GIVER`/`EPILOGUE` in `systems/quests.ts`, `ITEMS`/start gear in `systems/items.ts` (`setItems`), `SHOPS` in `systems/shops.ts`, `CHEST_LOOTS` in `systems/chests.ts`; all empty until then). **Goods (`items.json`, `parseItems`)**: `start` (the gear a new hero wears, an item id per slot, the weapon always; `newHero` reads `startGear()`), `items` (id → an `Item`: `name`, `value`, `slot` or `use`, `attack`/`defense`, the buffs, `rare`, `waters` (crops are watered with it, `waterWith`), `capacity` (bags), `ranged` {speed, range px, cooldown} (bows), `focus` {kind wand/tome, power, cooldown, `combos` [{keys, spell (a `SPELL_IDS` effect: what a spell *does* is engine code in `Casting.ts`, which weapon carries it and for how much is content), cost, power, count}]}, `look` (doll parts)), `shops` (id → name, tag, `keeper` (an npc entity), `greeting`, `stock` (item ids; never a `rare` one), `buys`: gear slots and `TRADE_KINDS` (`blade`, `bow`, `caster`, `supply`), `shopBuys`) and `loots` (chest contents: `{ item }`, `{ hp, mp? }`, `{ xp }` or `{ gold }`; the map's `z.chest` names one). The checks refuse an unknown field, a worn item that is also used, a bow/wand that is not a weapon, a combo with a repeated tap, an unknown spell or one hidden inside another combo or a skill's, an item named anywhere (start, stock, loot, quest) that the file lacks; `npm run map -- check` fails a chest loot or crop produce the map's campaign lacks, and `tools/content/campaigns.test.ts` holds every campaign's items to real parts in their slots and no spell or combo on two weapons. **Item codes** (the wire, `itemCode`) are an item's place in its campaign's list, so a campaign's file is append-only once players are on it; both co-op players play the same content, so they agree (no protocol change per campaign). Campaign 1's file holds the original 55 items and five shops, campaign 2 a short list of its own (no shops), the packs' goods are the `outfitters` pack. The static source is `staticContent(campaign)` (`content/<campaign>/<file>.json`; `assetVersions` hashes subfolders too). **Which world** (`content/campaignChoice.ts`, tested): `?campaign=<folder>` or `random` (default `DEFAULT_WORLD`, campaign 1, unless `VITE_CAMPAIGN` names another), `?seed=N` (alone, it means a random one) (a fresh seed is written into the address, so a reload or a shared link is the same world). **Packs and random campaigns** (`src/content/packs.ts`, Pixi-free, tested; `packContent(seed)` in `assets.ts` → `AssembledSource`): a pack (`public/content/packs/<id>.json`, listed in `packs/index.json`) holds any of `entities` (as in entities.json; a mob also has `tags`, its roles `common`/`hunter`/`ranged`/`brute`/`boss` and themes `fire`/`frost`/`swamp`/`undead`/`beast`, a `level` band and a `plural`), `keepers` (who gives a story: an npc entity, name, tag, the hub `homes` they live in), `regions` (a biome without links/ports/generation, a `level` band, `themes`, `hub` (has a `keeper` spot, can start a story), `terminal` (a dead end: one port, never in a story), its map's `ports` with the edge each is on (`{ "north": "north" }`, checked against the map; at most one per edge), the `foes` roles that live there, `roam` and `encounters` by role, its `sites`), **`pairings`** (how two regions meet: **two regions are only ever joined where a pairing names them**; its `tags` say what kind of meeting it is (`thaw`, `frost-creep`, `restless-dead`, `fire`...), `ports` optionally fix the doorways, and `lore` holds lines written for that pair, one line or one per region it is seen from: `way`, `sign`) and `arcs` (a story whose quests name roles, not ids: `"@r2.boss"` the mob picked for region 2's boss, `"@r1"` region 1's map, `"@r1.site"` its site kind; in text `{r1}`, `{r1.common}`/`.plural`/`.lower`, `{r1.site}`, `{keeper}`, and `{r1~r2.sign}` a lore line of the pairing the story crosses from r1 to r2, seen from r1; constraints: `slots` per region (`regions`, `themes`, extra `foes` tags such as a fire boss), `crossings` (`"r1~r2": { tags }` the pairing there must carry), `keepers`; a quest or chapter's `variants: [{ when: { "r1~r2": "<pairing id or tag>" | "r1": "<region>" or "theme:x" | "keeper": id }, ...fields }]` replace its fields for that meeting of biomes, which is how a story tells a detailed cross-biome quest). `assemble(seed, packs)` refuses packs `checkPacks` finds wrong (an id two packs both define (`mergePacks` records the clash; a pack never silently overrides another's), two regions drawing one map or owning one site kind, an arc switching an unknown capability, a pairing of an unknown region or port, a region nothing pairs with, a keeper's home that is not a hub, an arc quoting a pairing between regions that are not neighbours in its story), then picks an arc and the regions it needs (`storyFor`, built to scale to thousands of regions: the regions each slot could take are worked out once (an arc some slot of which nothing fits is dropped at once), the search walks only pairing partners (`cat.pairs`, an index by region; `pairingsBetween`, `partnersOf`), and it gives an arc up after `STORY_BUDGET` placements; `tools/content/packs.test.ts` assembles a made-up catalog of 3000 regions in well under 2 s: r1 a hub some allowed keeper calls home, the rest rising in level, each meeting its slot and able to fill the roles the arc names (`arcNeeds`), each step crossing a pairing that has the lore keys the text quotes and the tags the arc asks (`crossingNeeds`)), **lays the world out on a grid** (`Grid`: r1 at [0, 0], north is -y; a region goes in the cell its host's port leads to, the port it arrives by on the facing edge (`OPPOSITE`, `STEP`), so leaving north arrives in the south and **north, east, south and west through any doors comes home**; a cell is taken only if every region already beside it is paired with the newcomer, so neighbours always belong together), adds up to `MAX_SIDE_REGIONS` (3) side regions (only partners of a region already in, through a pairing, in a free cell; so the world's size never grows with the catalog), joins regions that ended up side by side where both have a facing port, then **bolts a dead end onto every open port one suits** (`terminal` regions, each once: Ember Cove, Frost Grotto, Bog Hollow, Old Shrine, built by `tools/map/deadEnd.ts`, a guard and a chest at the far end); a port still left open is sealed (below), picks a keeper and a mob per role (`candidates`: the role's tag (and the slot's extra tags), the band, and strictly one of the region's themes, so no fire brute guards the snow; a different mob per role) and writes ordinary campaign files, which `parseContent` validates like any others. Packs: `outfitters` (the goods every random campaign carries: `items`, `loots` and `start`, as in items.json; a pack may hold `items`, `shops` (their keepers come along) and `loots`, merged pack after pack with the same clash check, one pack says `start` and an arc may override it (`ArcDef.start`, a wizard's tale with a wand)), `emberfall` (campaign 2's mobs, Ysolde (at home on the coast), the coast (hub) and the caldera, their pairing `ash_trail`, the `ember_trail` arc: a fire region and a fire boss), `wilds` (goblins, wolfkin, shades, skeletons, zombies, the Barrow Lord, the Rimebound, the Fen Yeti, Keeper Ilsabet, the Mirewood (hub), Frostfen and Barrow, their pairings, the `lost_lanterns` arc: through a frost or swamp region to an undead one, over a `restless-dead` crossing) and `borderlands` (no regions of its own: the pairings across the other two packs, and `long_thaw`, a hub against a frost region whose quests change with the crossing: `thaw` from the coast, `frost-creep` from the Mirewood). Packs can be bridges: a pairing may name regions of other packs. Add a region: a `defineZone` map with `z.port`s (and a `z.giver('keeper', ...)` spot for a hub, `z.site`s for gather arcs), then its entry in a pack and a pairing (with `way` and `sign` lore) to each region it may sit beside; `npm run map -- build` writes its tilesets into the pack. **Ports** (`src/content/ports.ts`, tested): `z.port(name, col, row, w, arriveCol, arriveRow, h, seal)` marks a doorway whose far side the content decides (`MapData.ports`; left out of the file when a map has none): `w` x `h` cells (an east or west port is a column, `h` 3; doors and ports may span rows, `DoorSpawn.h`, `onDoor`), on the edge it is nearest (`side`, `portSide`), with a `seal`, the props (a rockfall, a thicket) that close it when nothing binds it (the map check fails a port without one); a biome's `ports` in biomes.json binds each to another map's port (both ways, `parseContent` checks; `npm run map -- check` fails one that does not join opposite edges), and `Zones.add` wires them into ordinary doors (`ContentManager.wire` → `wireDoors`; the arrival is the far port's `arrive`, `doorArrival`), blocking a port nothing binds and drawing its seal, so no open way ever leads nowhere. Campaign 2's coast and caldera are joined that way, so the same maps serve campaign 2 and the packs; the open-air world of campaign 1 has none. **Tests and tools**: `tools/content/disk.ts` reads a campaign from disk; the Vitest setup (`tools/content/setup.ts`) installs **campaign 1** (`DEFAULT_CAMPAIGN`), so every existing rule test runs on campaign 1, and `tools/content/campaigns.test.ts` holds every campaign's entities to the same rules (real parts, fair enemies). The map tools check each map with the campaign whose biomes draw from it installed (`withCampaignOf`; a pack region's map with the packs' catalog, `PACKS` = every region at once with no story, `catalogCampaign`), and `npm run map -- check` checks every campaign's content, the packs' catalog and the random campaigns of seeds 1-40 (`checkCampaigns`; a map no campaign or pack uses fails); `tools/content/packs.test.ts` assembles 150 seeds and holds each to the game's own checks, a story that starts in a hub, rises in level and can be walked port to port, every role and name filled in, every join a pairing, the arc's slots, crossings and keepers honoured, the grid consistent (every door to the next cell its way and the facing edge; a random walk through doors always stands where the layout says), every dead end used, and every port left open sealed. The browser tests build campaign 1 (`playwright.config.ts`, `E2E_CAMPAIGN` overrides). Maps stay in `maps/` and `public/maps/` for every campaign (campaign 2's are `emberfall_coast`/`emberfall_caldera`, standalone `defineZone` open-air zones joined by an iris door); give a new campaign's zones a prefix so names never clash. **Who stands in a zone** is the content's, never the map's: `Zones.add` asks `ContentManager.populate(zone, map, node)`, which gives the zone's scenario node's placements (mobs first, then the biome's roamers; folk apart) in a fixed order, and `ZoneState.populate` builds them (and replaces them, destroying the old ones, when another node is loaded over the zone). A zone shows the node named after its biome unless a scenario was loaded over it (`Zones.assign`/`nodeOf`). **`loadScenario(nodeId)`** (`OverworldScene.loadScenario` → `scenes/overworld/Scenario.ts`): loads the node's biome's zone, takes the local player there through the iris (the old view is cleared away) to the node's `spawn` or the map's, puts the node's entities in, and shows its choices in `hud/Choices.ts` (`#choices`, a column of buttons at the right; a choice the hero does not meet is disabled with "Needs Level 4"; picking one loads `next`). Campaign 1's nodes are one per zone (named after it, no choices) plus a sample `greenwood_ambush` (dev: `__game.overworld.loadScenario('greenwood_ambush')`); campaign 2 (`?campaign=campaign2`) starts in `landing`, whose choices lead on ("Make camp" → `cinder_coast`, "Climb straight to the caldera" needs level 4). **Checks**: `npm run map -- check` (`tools/content/check.ts`) fails a biome whose map is missing, whose tilesets or climate differ from its map, a map with no biome, a port binding naming a port a map does not have, `links` that differ from the (wired) doors, a placement or spawn off open ground of its own biome (blocked, a door, the neighbour's margin), a gather quest whose zone has fewer sites than it asks for and a kill quest whose mob lives nowhere it says; it warns when a roam rule cannot place them all. **Co-op**: both players must play the same world: the hello's `build` carries it after a `#` (`setContentTag` in `net/session.ts`, from `CampaignChoice.tag`, e.g. `random:42`), and a friend in another world moves to the host's world by itself (see Co-op: the lobby and joining); the join link keeps the address's `?campaign`/`?seed` (`hud/Multiplayer.ts`). **Scenarios: solo only for now.** The host does not send a node: online, `Scenario.update` puts every zone back to its biome's own node and hides the choices, and `loadScenario` refuses. Because populations are deterministic from the content and the map, a guest builds the same mobs in the same order as the host (snapshots refer to mobs by index) with nothing on the wire; when the host comes to dictate the node, it must send `node_id` per zone and the guest `Zones.assign` it.
- **A world from a log (`src/content/world.ts`, Pixi-free, tested in `tools/content/world.test.ts`; content)** — a world is a seed, the packs, and an append-only **log** of fully resolved entries (`LogEntry`): `begin` (the arc, its keeper, the hub r1 and its foes), `step` (the story's next region: the pairing crossed, the ports, the mob for each foe role), `side` (a side region or a dead end beside a region already in) and `join` (a door between neighbours). `World.apply` checks each entry against the packs' rules (the arc's slot, rising level, a pairing serving that step, a free port leading to a grid cell whose neighbours all pair with it, foes that fit their role) and refuses it without changing anything (`WorldError`); `replay(log)` rebuilds a world with no random numbers, so every co-op player and every later solver agrees. `files()` writes ordinary campaign files for the world as it stands: an unfinished story carries its quests in order up to the first that names a region not yet placed (`told()`, by the regions a quest, its variants and its chapter's intro reach), and the epilogue only once complete. **`assemble` (random campaigns) is an automatic Dungeon Master on top of it**: it finds a whole story (`storyFor`), writes its steps, side regions, joins and dead ends as entries, and returns the log with the files (`Assembly.log`). **A live world** is written during play: `open(arc?)` begins it and places as many steps as its first chapter needs; then `options()` offers the next step as **story cues** (`StoryOption`: a kind of land and of crossing, "Into frost lands, over a thaw crossing"; never a map), only those after which the rest of the arc can still be told (`storyFor` resumed from the story so far, `nextPlacements`), and `choose(cue)` resolves one (seeded by the world's seed and the step, so it is the same on every page) for `apply`. The maps are the solver's: an "endless desert" is a chain of different desert regions, so more regions per theme in the packs is what makes longer stories possible. **In play** (`?campaign=live&seed=N`, optionally `&arc=<id>`; `campaignChoice.ts` `LIVE`, tag `live:N[:arc]`): `LiveSource` (`assets.ts` `liveContent`) opens the World and serves its files, and the boot preloads every pack entity's look (`worldLooks`, `loadSprites(_, extra)`) since the story may bring any of them. `scenes/overworld/Story.ts` grows it: whoever may choose (`canChoose`: solo, the player as their own DM, which is how a story is tried out; online only the host, the DM) picks a cue on the **story card** (`hud/DmCard.ts`, offered by `scenes/overworld/DmPrompts.ts` when the party has finished what was told, or on demand from the DM bar's Story button: the roads as buttons; textContent only), and each new entry is applied (`World.apply`), the content reloaded (`ContentManager.reload`: everything but the goods, which a live world never changes and whose item objects heroes hold) and **the ports it binds open in zones already loaded** (`Zones.rewire`: `openPort` in `content/ports.ts` gives the door, the cells its seal blocked and the seal's objects, which fade, `MapView.setObjectAlpha`; nothing else in the zone changes), with "The way opens" over the doorway and the new land's name in the `AreaBanner` on every page. **Co-op**: the host sends its friends the log a few entries at a time, round and round, newest first when one is added (`WorldMsg`, `WORLD_CHUNK` 3, every 0.4 s; protocol v30), and a friend applies the entries that follow on from its own (it opened the same world from the same address, so its opening matches; one that does not fit marks it astray). Not yet: quests run out at the end of what is placed until the DM chooses the next step. **With today's packs every step offers one road** (too few regions and pairings to branch): the choice is real in the engine and grows with the packs' regions per theme and pairings.
