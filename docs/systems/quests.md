# Story quests and the cast

Read this before touching quests, givers, sites, guidance or the journal (`src/systems/quests.ts`, `guide.ts`, `QuestLog`). Detail moved out of the root `CLAUDE.md` (which has the layers, commands, procedure and conventions); words such as "above" or "below" inside these bullets refer to this file or the map in the root. Keep the bullet for a feature up to date in the same change.

- **Story as a cast, not a task list (quests v2)** — `Quest.giver`/`turnIn` name who offers and who takes a quest in (default: the chain's `giver`), so one story passes through many people (`offererOf`/`completerOf`; campaign 1: Maren, Orin, Captain Hale, Gruk, Brannoch, Zahra, Voss; `talk(hero, giverId)` answers any giver, and a giver who is not part of the current step says one of its `idle` lines, key `<giver>.idle`). Objectives beyond kill/gather/bring: `meet` (done on taking it; the turn-in person is the point) and `visit` (walk into a `z.place` of a zone: `recordVisit`, `VISIT_RADIUS`, called from `Simulation.updatePlayer`; `tools/content/check.ts` fails a place the map lacks and a giver nobody placed with `z.giver`). Campaign 1's 13 quests in four chapters end in story bosses placed by scenarios.json (Pale Lurker, Urgath the Tusk Captain, the Stillheart). Co-op: unchanged on the wire (the host decides; guests read the same story keys and quest log).
- **Story quests (campaign 1: Lanternrest's Warden Maren; campaign 2: Keeper Ysolde; a random campaign: its arc's, told by its keeper, giver id `keeper`)** — `systems/quests.ts` (Pixi-free, tested; the rules, the data is each campaign's `quests.json`): a linear chain `QUESTS` (the order is the story; part one of it: the Stillness, the broken Great Hourglass, the clockmaker) given one at a time by a giver (`GIVERS`, placed with `z.giver(id, col, row)` → `MapData.givers`; Maren stands on the town market). Objectives: `kill` (a `MOB_KINDS` key, optionally only in a zone), `gather` (touch N different *sites*) and `bring` (hand over pack items). Easy early quests pay gold and XP; from the fifth on they also pay a rare buffed piece (`rare` items at the end of campaign 1's items.json: Wayfarer's Charm, Warden's Mantle, Duneglass Boots, Tuskbreaker Helm, Sunforged Cuirass, Hourglass Pendant), which goes on if it beats what is worn, else into the pack; a full pack refuses the reward and changes nothing. Each hero has `hero.quests` (`QuestLog`: `stage` done, `taken`, `progress` = kills or a bit per site). Talking (`talk(hero)`) offers, reminds or pays; the dialog is `linesFor(key)`. **Told as a story**: `CHAPTERS` (four, each with an `intro` Maren says before its first quest's offer, `offerLines`/`startsChapter`) group the quests; each quest has `learned` (one line of lore, collected in the journal shown as "The story so far" on the Quests tab, `journal`) and `fieldNote` (narration shown as a dialog the moment the task is done out in the world). `OverworldScene.tellStory` reads the local hero's log (so it is the same for solo, host and guest, nothing on the wire) and shows the field note, a chapter title card (`AreaBanner`, "Chapter N") when a chapter's first quest is taken, and "To be continued..." at the end, each only when no dialog or menu is open. **Sites** (`SITE_KINDS`: glimmercap, echostone, hoursand; `z.site(kind, col, row)` → `MapData.sites`, drawn by `entities/QuestSite.ts`) are walkable spots shown only to a player whose current quest wants them (`siteOpen`, local view); the action key near one reads its note (the n-th touch plays the n-th note) and `OverworldScene.act` → `touchSite` counts it. Kills credit everyone in the zone (`Simulation.hitMob` → `recordKill`). The Menu has a Quests tab (`hud/QuestLog.ts`) and the giver shows "!" (a task to give) or "?" (ready to hand in) over their head. **Guidance** (all local to the player, nothing on the wire): every quest has a `hint` (shown on the Quests tab) and `where` (zones it can be done in, plus the shop `door` for a bring task); `systems/guide.ts` (`guideFor`, `trackerText`, Pixi-free, tested) turns the hero's quest and a plain description of the zone (`GuideWorld`) into a target: the giver, the nearest wanted mob or open site here, or, for a far zone, the door leading toward it (`systems/places.ts` `nextZone` over `ZONE_LINKS` (each biome's `links` in biomes.json, which `npm run map -- check` checks against the built maps' doors)). A *person or door* (known place) gets an arrow (`entities/GuideArrow.ts`, screen space, steady, with the area name); something to be *found* (a mob, a plant, a stone) gets none: `guideFor` returns an off-centre **search area** (`Guide.radius` 16 cells, `searchMiddle`: its middle snaps to a coarse 10-cell grid with a fixed 4-cell push, so it holds still and always contains the target), drawn on the map overlay as a dashed gold circle with a "?" (a star for an arrow target; `MapOverlay.setGuide`). The quest page shows a picture of the target (`hud/questArt.ts`: a monster portrait from `Wardrobe.portrait`, the site's shape, or the item's icon; `Menu.portrait` is set in `main.ts`). `Hud` shows the one-line tracker (`#quest-tracker`). **Names**: each biome's `name` in its campaign's biomes.json (installed as `ZONE_NAMES`, `systems/places.ts`) is the one place an area is named (Lanternrest, The Greenwood, The Sunscar Desert...) and quest text must use those names (`guide.test.ts` checks hints and rejects old names); entering a zone shows `AreaBanner` (big title top middle, "New area discovered" the first time, once the door transition ends), and the map overlay (`MapOverlay`) is headed with the zone name and labels each `MapData.places` entry (`z.place(name, col, row)` in a biome, world cells) once the player has explored that cell. The chain, givers, site kinds, chapters and epilogue are content (`quests.json` of the campaign, installed into `QUESTS`, `GIVERS`... at boot); add a quest there (and sites to a biome with `z.site`); the tests check every objective, item and site exists. **Co-op**: the host owns every `QuestLog`; solo/host talk and touch directly, a guest sends `do` op `talk` (shop = giver id) and `act` presses, the host checks it is beside the giver and answers with `say.story` (the key, turned into lines on the guest) and the log in every `pack` message (`PackMsg.quests`, `questWire`/`questFromWire`); progress floats are ordinary text events.
