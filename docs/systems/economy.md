# Inventory, economy, shops and chests

Read this before touching the pack, gear, shops, trading goods or chests. Detail moved out of the root `CLAUDE.md` (which has the layers, commands, procedure and conventions); words such as "above" or "below" inside these bullets refer to this file or the map in the root. Keep the bullet for a feature up to date in the same change.

- **Inventory** — `systems/inventory.ts` (Pixi-free, tested): `hero.pack` holds carried items, `BASE_PACK` (10) slots plus the `capacity` of the worn `bag` (sack +4, backpack +8; `packSize`). `equipFromPack`/`unequip`/`dropFromPack`/`dropWorn` either succeed or change nothing and return a reason (the weapon can only be swapped; a bag cannot come off or shrink while the pack needs its room). A one-handed non-bow weapon fits the off-hand too (it adds attack; it has no off-hand art, so `playerOutfit` ignores its look). The `I` key (the `inventory` action; the HUD `I` button on touch) opens the menu on the Inventory tab (`hud/Inventory.ts`: equipped slots, pack grid, tap to pick then Equip / Equip off-hand / Take off / Drop, double click wears or removes; icons are inline SVG in `hud/itemIcons.ts`, since items have no art of their own). The scene attaches `InventoryHandlers` on `enter`. Dropped items are `entities/GroundItem.ts`; the action key picks one up (before swinging). Inventory actions are data (`InvOp`: equip, unequip, use, dropPack, dropWorn; `applyInvOp`; wire form `encodeOp`/`decodeOp`), so the screen only calls `InventoryHandlers.apply`. Solo and host do them on the local hero (`OverworldScene.doInvOp`); a co-op guest does them on its own copy at once *and* sends the oldest unconfirmed one in `InputMsg.inv` (numbered 1, 2, 3...). The host does each once, in order, on that friend's hero (`applyPeerInv`) and confirms with `PackMsg.ack`; a guest ignores the host's gear and pack while any action is unconfirmed. Items on the ground live in each `ZoneState`, ride in that zone's snapshot (`SnapMsg.ground`, each with the seconds it has left; at most `MAX_GROUND` = 16 per zone, so a drop is refused beyond that), and any player's action key picks up the nearest one. A dropped item crumbles away after `GROUND_LIFETIME` (3 minutes) and blinks for the last `GROUND_WARNING` seconds: solo and the host age and remove them (`ageGround`, in each occupied zone's simulation, with a puff); a guest only follows the host's list and times. A guest's own items on the ground are set aside on joining and put back on leaving (`stashGround`/`restoreGround`). Not saved between visits (the hero never is).
- **Economy: gold, pack and shops** — `Hero` has `gold`; the pack is the Inventory's `hero.pack` (capped, see above), and supplies like potions live in it. Every `Item` has a `value` in gold (the campaign's items.json, see Content); supplies (`potion`, `elixir`) have no slot and a `use` (HP/MP restored) and are drunk with the Inventory screen's Drink button. The shop-side pack moves are at the end of `systems/inventory.ts`: `wearIfBetter` (puts a piece on and sends the old one to the pack), `wearFromPack`, `useFromPack`, `packEntries` (grouped), `isBetter`/`upgrade`. **Money comes mainly from enemies**: each `MobKind` has `gold`, rolled ±30% by `rollGold`; `OverworldScene.hitMob` gives every player the same purse on a kill (like XP) and floats `+Ng`. Chests can also hold gold (`coins`/`purse`/`hoard`). `systems/shops.ts` (Pixi-free, tested): `SHOPS` (content, items.json `shops`): in campaign 1 five specialists, each with a `keeper` (an `NPC_LOOKS` key), `stock` and what it `buys` (`shopBuys`) (a shop buys back only its own trade, at `SELL_RATE` 40% of `value`): `smithy` (swords, shields), `armory` (helms, chest, legs), `ranger` (bows, boots, cloak), `apothecary` (potions, elixirs), `wizard` (wands and tomes, bought back too; the smithy no longer takes them). `buy` wears gear that beats what the hero has, else puts it in the pack, and refuses (taking no gold) when there is no free pack slot. **Chests no longer hold what a shop sells**; they keep the rare pieces (Viking Helm, Legion Armor, Parrying Dagger, elixir) and purses. Keep a piece in one place: stock in one shop, or chests. Shopkeepers stand inside the shop rooms (`z.shop(id, col, row)`, `MapData.shops`, checked by `tools/map/check.ts`); the building outside is `z.prop('smithy'|...)` with a `z.door` into the room. In `OverworldScene` the action key within `KEEPER_REACH` (2.3 cells, across the counter) of a keeper opens a greeting `Dialog` (its `say(lines, onDone)` runs `onDone` after the last line), then `hud/ShopCounter.ts` (`#shop` in `index.html`: Buy/Sell tabs, Wear/Drink buttons) — a modal like the menu: solo it pauses the world, online it only freezes the hero. The shop counter's Sell tab also has Wear/Drink buttons (`ShopCounter.onGearChange` makes the scene redraw the hero). `Hud` shows gold, the Status tab too. **In co-op every player is their own character**: the host keeps each friend's whole `Hero` (stats, gold, pack, gear) and is the only one who changes it. A friend's wishes travel as `do` messages (buy/sell/wear/use + an item code, built by `systems/trader.ts`'s `remoteTrader`; the menu and shop counter talk to a `Trader` and don't know whether it is `localTrader` or remote); the host's `handleTrades`/`tradeFor` check them (known item and shop, the friend in that shop's room within reach of its keeper) and answer with a `pack` message (gold, worn item codes, pack, plus `say` = the reply to show once). `pack` goes to each friend on change and once a second, and `looks` (what every player wears) goes to all, so everyone sees everyone's gear. Item codes are `itemCode`/`itemFromCode` in `systems/inventory.ts` (an item's index in the campaign's items.json plus one): adding an item at the end of a campaign's list is safe, reordering it is a protocol change for that campaign. A guest's Inventory screen works like the host's (its actions go to the host as `InputMsg.inv`, see Inventory); a friend's character exists only for that game: their solo hero is put back when they leave (`OverworldScene.solo`).
- **Treasure chests** — `systems/chests.ts` (Pixi-free: `CHEST_KINDS` = rows of `public/tilesets/chests.png`, `CHEST_LOOTS` (the campaign's items.json `loots`), `collect(hero, loot)`: gear is worn if its slot is empty or it is an upgrade (the old piece goes to the pack), otherwise it goes to the pack, and a full pack leaves the chest shut; potions, XP and gold also work) and `entities/Chest.ts` (open/closed sprite, origin at feet). Zones place them with `z.chest(kind, loot, col, row)` (solid cell; `MapData.chests`, checked by `tools/map/check.ts`). In `OverworldScene` the action key opens a closed chest within `CHEST_REACH` before it swings (`act`); only solo/host decide, and the host sends opened chests to guests as a bitmask (`SnapMsg.chests`, max 30 per map). Guest gear sprites are not synced.
