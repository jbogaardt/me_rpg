# towercraft: a small real-time strategy game on a single road

A separate game on the same site (built into `dist/towercraft/`, see the root `CLAUDE.md`). It shares no code with Me RPG; only
the conventions (Vite, strict TypeScript, Pixi-free rules with unit tests beside them, Prettier, ESLint).

## The game

- **One road, two castles, five maps** (`greenwood` the classic and the sim's default, `frostlands` the menu's default, `desert`, `mire`, `ember`; picked on the title screen, kept in `localStorage` as `towercraft:map`). Units walk the road (175-225 cells, 2-7 cells a second) and meet in between. The map is `Match.board` (`Board` in `board.ts`: road, castles, `roadCells`, `sceneryCells`, `pointAt`, `footOf`), so the rules never read a map constant. Destroy the other castle to win (or, after `TIME_LIMIT`, castle health left). Only the castle must fall; razing other buildings weakens the foe.
- **The dock and the card** (Me RPG's menu kit, `index.html`): tabs Units / Build (Economy, Production, Defence, Support) / Upgrades; tap a thumbnail for a *card* with stats and the purchase button (a bunker card loads soldiers; the castle card upgrades).
- **Two currencies, wood and stone** (`Cost`, `price()`; `Player.wood/stone`): everything costs both. The castle pays `CastleDef.income[level]` every `PAY_EVERY` (5 s); mills (wood) and quarries (stone) (`TowerDef.econ`, `limit` 3, a picture per race) add more; razing one cuts the foe's income (`incomeOf`). **Purchases are instant**: no queue, train time or cooldown; only money and `UNIT_CAP` (120) limit spam.
- **The castle sells only its base unit; producer buildings unlock the rest** (`UnitDef.needs`, `TowerDef.trains`, `unitUnlocked`, `towerUnlocked`): Goblin Hatchery/Den/Aerie/Roost, Dwarf Hall/Pen/Eyrie/Sky Forge, Germanic Barracks/Stables/Workshop/Falconry/Roost. A building may need another (`TowerDef.needs`) and be limited (`limit`). Razing one locks its units again.
- **Attack styles** (`UnitDef`): `range` (long range hits across to another run of the road), `splash` (a blast or trample; also hurts towers if the attacker may fight them), `multi` (one blow strikes that many foes: a sweep or flame), `suicide` (dies on its first strike, no bounty), `siege` (damage x vs towers and castle), `hitsAir`, `cloak`. Flyers (`air`) **follow the road** over everything; only air-capable things hurt them.
- **Units leave the road to take a tower** (`detourFor`, `Board.footOf`, `Unit.off`): out of reach from the road, a unit steps off at the road point straight across from the tower (square to the road there, never a slant, up to `DETOUR_MAX` cells), fights, and returns when the tower falls or the order is `bypass`.
- **Orders** (`setOrder`): units `attack` or `bypass` the towers and units they meet, separately; the castle is always attacked once in reach.
- **Defence**: towers shoot units only. *Attackers* hit `ground`, `air` or `both` (`splash`, poison `dot`); *auras* `power` (at most double) or `heal`; *detectors* reveal cloaked units; **bunkers** (`TowerDef.bunker`) are loaded by `loadBunker` with ONE garrison-capable ranged soldier per slot for its share of the price; each shoots from the bunker with its own stats, range+`rangeBonus` and upgrades, and dies with it. `damage: 0` never shoots.
- **Upgrades** (`UpgradeDef`): per race ground/air x damage/health, three levels of +15% (`boostOf`), bought at a smithy-type building (`buyUpgrade`, instant). Health applies to units bought later (`Unit.maxHp`), damage at once.
- **Build reach**: free cells inside the castle's reach (6, 9, 13) or a tower's `reach`; castle levels unlock tier 2-3.
- **Fog of war** (`visionOf`, `canSee`, `isSeen`): castles, towers and units have a `sight` radius; the build area is permanent revealed ground; **unit sight is temporary** and never extends it. Hidden enemies are not drawn, targeted or splashed; cloaked ones need sight *and* a detector. The AI reads the foe through vision too. `main.ts` draws the fog as a tiny canvas scaled up smooth.
- **Look** (`main.ts`, cosmetic): units drawn at `SIZE` x2; walk, stand, strike (from `Shot.by`), tower fx, role badges, pay-day figures, a quiet blast on `Death.boom`. Keep it quiet: no sparkles.
- **Races** (`races.ts`): swarm/Goblins (fast, weak, in groups, boomers, bomb bats, thieves), titan/Dwarves (slow, strong, thunderers, flame runesmiths, gryphons), legion/Germanic (archers, sweeping saxons, sappers, catapults, falcons, griffins): 4-6 ground and 2-3 air units each. The `races` tests check the tech tree is closed.

## Code

- **Rules are Pixi-free and deterministic** (`src/sim/`, enforced by ESLint): `sim.ts` the match, `races.ts` the data, `board.ts` the maps, `ai.ts` the seeded opponent, `testkit.ts` test helpers. `main.ts` draws a `Match`, owns the camera and turns taps into commands. `?debug` exposes the match to playtest scripts. The rules can later run on a server.
- **Art** is Wyrmsun's, copied by `tools/towercraftArt.ts` from a clone of its repo (`public/art/CREDITS.md`): unit sheets CC-BY-SA 3.0 (the title screen credits them; the terms follow the art), CC0 castles, towers, buildings. `towercraftArtCheck.test.ts` checks every art id resolves. Fields (`art/maps/<id>.png`) come from `npm run towercraft:board [-- <id>]` (`tools/towercraftBoard.ts`), which also writes `src/sim/scenery.ts`; roads are `src/sim/road.ts`. **A new map** is a `ROADS` entry, a `THEMES` entry and a generator run; `board.test.ts` checks fairness. Only `greenwood` is turned half way round onto itself; sides alternate in balance runs.
- Headless screenshots: `--use-angle=swiftshader`.

## State of play

Done: five maps, upgrades, combat, fog, orders, card UI, animation; the wood/stone economy, producers, bunkers, unit upgrades, attack styles, flyers on the road, off-road tower attacks.

**Lab and balance**: `npm run towercraft:lab -- --help` (`tools/towercraftLab.ts`: maps x races x `STRATEGIES`, win rates, CSV, `--set swarm.units.glider.hp=*1.2` what-ifs) and `npm run towercraft:balance [-- N [seedBase]]`; re-run after changing `races.ts`, `ai.ts` or `sim.ts` constants (`balance.test.ts` is a guard only). Last, greenwood, N 40: 95% decisive, median 8.2 min, Goblins 54%, Dwarves 46%, Germanic 50%. Levers: income (mills +6 wood, quarries +8 stone), prices, hp, count, speed, splash. Keep both incomes high: scarce stone piled up wood and the Dwarves stalled. **Miss:** Dwarf mirrors time out about a third of the time (the AI keeps at most `MAX_TOWERS` = 6 defences); a 10% price change swung results 30 points, so judge by N of 100.

**The AI** (`Style` drawn from its seed): mills and quarries first (`ecoEvery`), tech (`techStart`, `techEvery`), waves of at least `MIN_WAVE` units (bombs against towers, sweeps against crowds, archers against air), upgrades from spare money, bunkers it loads, castle levels, towers as far forward as they see road, a detector/shooter when the foe fields cloaked/air, bypass when it could kill the castle fast. It reads the foe only through `foeUnits`, `foeTowers`, `foeCastle`.

Next: a *beacon* tower; detector units; slow and armour; upgrades per unit type; rally and attack-move; online; mines to hold on the road; tune the Dwarf mirror.
