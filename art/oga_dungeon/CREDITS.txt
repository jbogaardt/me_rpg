Cave, sewer and desert tilesets, from OpenGameArt.org. Drawn on a 16px tile grid; tools/map/dungeonSheets.ts doubles
them (nearest neighbour, nothing else changed) to the game's 32px cell, into public/tilesets/ (dungeon_cave.png,
dungeon_sewer.png, dunes.png).
