# src/entities (things drawn in the world)

Keep it up to date in the same change ("above"/"below" in the text mean this file or the root map).

- **`src/entities/`** — `Character.ts` renders the hero/NPCs/mobs from a composed *doll sheet* (below): looping idle/walk follow `animate(dt, moving)`, one-shot `play('slash'|'cast'|'hurt', ms?)` takes over until done, `setSheet()` swaps the look when gear changes. Its container origin is the *feet*, so collision and y-sorting (`zIndex = y`) use it directly. `Mob.ts`: enemy AI state machine (idle/wander → chase the nearest hero → telegraphed windup → strike → recover; stagger on hit; fade and respawn at home), driven through the `MobWorld` interface the scene passes in (`strike` for melee, `shoot` for projectiles); pacing is per kind (`windup`/`recover`/`stagger` via `mobTimings`, so tough kinds shrug off stun and quick ones swing fast); **a hero cannot juggle a mob**: after one stagger it shrugs off further stagger for `POISE` (0.9 s; the damage still lands, it is only shoved) and past half its windup a blow cannot be interrupted, so it always gets to swing back, and **a melee blow cannot be dodged by stepping back and forth** (core engine, `Mob.ts`): it starts winding up from `REACH` (32 px, more than the sword's own reach, so a hero close enough to poke is close enough to be swung at), each windup is ±20% of the kind's (`WINDUP_SPREAD`, so the rhythm can't be learned), it turns to follow the hero until the windup is committed and then locks its aim, and the blow is a real lunge along that aim (`lungeOf`: quick kinds leap up to 34 px, slow ones 14; the body moves, so a guest sees it from the snapshots with nothing new on the wire) that hits whoever is in the circle in front of it (`HIT_AHEAD`/`HIT_RADIUS`); a small step back is caught, a sidestep once the aim is locked or an early full retreat dodges, and a blow that misses leaves it busy only `WHIFF_RECOVER` (40%) of its recover before it presses in. A mob that loses the hero mends (`MEND`, a quarter of its HP a second while idle or wandering), so a hero can't chip at it, run off to heal and come back, and a kind with a `ray` (the desert **Mummy**) is a caster with a melee fallback: within `REACH` it swings (`slash`); from `ray.range` (4.5 cells) it thrusts its sceptre (windup), then enters the `channel` state: it faces left or right (the sheet has no up/down attack art) and holds the sceptre-thrust frame (`Character.hold`/`release`, `RAY_POSE` in `Mob.ts`, which also records where the sceptre head is) while a pink beam runs from that head to the hero that burns every `ray.tick` s for `ray.power` x its attack (`MobWorld.ray` → `Simulation.mobRay`: a damage-over-time `harm(..., burn)`: no knockback, stun or invulnerability, spared while `safe`). The beam ends when `ray.duration` runs out, the hero gets past 1.4x the range, a wall comes between them, or **any hit on the mummy** (`Mob.hit`, however poised: it is staggered); then `ray.rest` s of melee only (`mobAttack(kind, dist, rayReady)`). **Co-op**: `channel` is a `MOB_STATES` entry (protocol v25) and every client draws the beam itself from that state to the nearest hero (`Mob.drawBeam`, a guest uses `Mob.setRemote(view, heroes)`); the host alone burns. **Giants**: `MobKind.scale` (the Abominable Snowman is 2) draws a kind bigger (`Character.setScale`, `Character.size`) and the rules follow it: `Body.size` widens its collision foot box and its body-vs-body blocking (`systems/collision.ts`), its melee reach and strike distance grow with it (`mobAttack`), the hero's sword and arrows hit a bigger circle, and its health bar and floating numbers ride higher. A kind with a `shot` is a ranged attacker (stops at range, backs off when crowded, telegraphs with the cast pose, then fires a slow `foeBolt` the scene flies and tests against the players); on a co-op guest the same class is only a view of the host's state (`setRemote` also plays its windup and hurt poses). `effects.ts`: transient world visuals (slash arc, bolt, floating `BitmapText` numbers) owned by an `Effects` list. `ParticleEffect.ts`: draws a data-defined particle effect (`ParticleContainer`, one per emitter) as an `Effect`; spawn with `particleEffect('name', x, y, { dir })`. Character theme: a boy stranded outside time.
- **Ambient life**: `src/entities/ambient.ts` is passive background animation driven by body movement and map metadata (no hooks into collision): footprints that alternate feet and fade within 2-3 s, only on soft terrain (`PRINTS`: `sand`, `snow`, and `puddle` once a puddle ground exists; hero and mobs; pooled, capped at 96), and leaves that drop when the hero moves behind a canopy (a `tree`/`bush`-tagged object drawn over the hero) or swings at one (`Ambient.shake`, called from `OverworldScene.attack`). Leaf colours follow the canopy's sheet name (`trees_green/frost/dry/jungle/dead/swamp.png`, `PALETTES`). It parents everything to `world.layer`. To add an effect, keep it pooled and capped, and key it off `surface`/`tag`/`level` rather than coordinates.
