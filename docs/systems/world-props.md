# Props and scenery in play: barrels, signs, pets, ice

Read this before touching movable barrels, signposts, pets or ice. Detail moved out of the root `CLAUDE.md` (which has the layers, commands, procedure and conventions); words such as "above" or "below" inside these bullets refer to this file or the map in the root. Keep the bullet for a feature up to date in the same change.

- **Signposts at the forks of the roads** — `systems/signs.ts` (Pixi-free, tested): a biome spec calls `z.sign(col, row, ways)` at each fork of its trails (`ways`: `up`/`down`/`left`/`right` → a zone key, named through `ZONE_NAMES`, or a `z.place` name on the same map); in a biome it stands on the free cell nearest that point that is beside a road but not on it (`BiomeWindow.sign`), as the solid `signpost` prop (drawn by `tools/map/interior.ts` on `interior.png`), and lands in `MapData.signs`. `checkMap` fails a sign with fewer than two ways, an unknown destination, or no reachable cell beside it. A sign is read **like talking to an NPC**: in `OverworldScene.handleAction` the action key within `SIGN_REACH` (1.5 cells of its foot; `signNear`) opens it whichever way the hero faces (before shopkeepers, givers, pets and the NPC, and before a swing), and the hero turns to face the board; the dialog is one page, a line per way with its arrow and compass point (`signLines`, "↑ North: The Barrow Lands"; `#dialog p` keeps the line breaks). Every fork of the open-air world has one (the town's streets end in the market, and the desert and the Mirewater have one trail each). **Co-op**: local reading of map data like the lectern; nothing on the wire.
- **Pets (dogs, cats and a rat)** — `systems/pets.ts` (Pixi-free, tested): `PET_KINDS` (four dogs on `public/sprites/pets/dogs.png`, four cats on `cats.png`; 32px frames, four columns per animal, rows right/up/down/left, column 3 = lying; the `rat` on `rat.png` has 3 columns, rows down/left/right/up, no lying frame, its tail twitches through `idleFrames`: each kind carries its own `rows`/`cols`), what each says (`petSound`: dogs woof, cats meow) and `petPose`/`petFrame`. `PetBrain` (same file, Pixi-free) is their behaviour: stand, lie down (not the rat), or amble to a spot within `LEASH` (5 cells) of where they were placed, each step checked against the zone's collision (`kind.speed`). `entities/Pet.ts` draws one; `attend` stops it and turns it to the player. Zones place them with `z.pet(kind, col, row)` (`MapData.pets`, checked by `tools/map/check.ts`; they do not block); the town has ten, every biome one to two (dogs, cats, and rats in the swamps and jungle). In `OverworldScene.handleAction` the action key within 1.5 cells of a pet makes it answer in the dialog (before the NPC, after shopkeepers). **Co-op: pets are local scenery like the NPC** (each player's pets wander independently, so they are not in the same spot on two screens; the talk is a local dialog; nothing goes over the wire). Art source/licence: see CREDITS.md.
- **Movable barrels (core engine + the `carrying` capability, co-op)** — `systems/barrels.ts` (Pixi-free, tested), `entities/Barrel.ts`, `scenes/overworld/Barrels.ts`. A map object tagged `barrel` (a `Prefab.movable` in the catalog) is left **unblocked** by the map builder; `ZoneState` hides its drawn copy (`MapView.setObjectAlpha`) and stands a `Barrel` entity in its place (`barrelSpots`, numbered in object order, so every page agrees). A loose barrel is a `Body` (`BARREL_SIZE` 2) in `Arena.bodies()`, so it blocks players and mobs through the usual body-vs-body rules wherever it is put. The action key beside one (`BARREL_REACH`, not while a foe is near: then it swings) picks it up and it is held out in front of the carrier (`heldSpot`, `Barrel.hold`: drawn over them, or behind when they face up; blocks nobody); pressed again it is set down in front (`dropSpot`: where it fits the map and overlaps no body, else "No room here"). `Barrels.update` (solo/host, every zone) moves carried barrels with their carrier and sets one down where it was if the carrier left. A zone remembers moved barrels while unloaded (`ZoneMemory.barrels`). **Co-op**: the host decides (a guest's action press reaches `act` like a chest's); `SnapMsg.barrels` lists the ones away from home or carried (`barrelsWire`: number, x, y, 0 or 1 + the carrier's facing; absent = all home; protocol v41) and a guest only draws them. Gated by the zone-scoped `carrying` capability at `Barrels.pickUp` (off: barrels stay where they stand and still block).
- **Slippery ice**: `systems/ice.ts` (Pixi-free, tested): `isIce` reads the `ice` surface under a player's feet and `stepVelocity` eases `Player.vel` toward the held direction (grip `ICE_ACCEL`) and coasts when keys are released (`ICE_DRAG`); off ice velocity equals the walking velocity, so normal movement is unchanged. Applied to players in `OverworldScene.move` (host/solo; guests only see positions); mobs ignore it. The ice tiles are painted (cracks, glints) by `tools/map/recolor.ts` into the empty corner of `frost_ground.png`; the lake's ring cells keep the snow surface, so only the inner fill is slippery.
